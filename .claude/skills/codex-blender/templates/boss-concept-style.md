
## Boss style (the same toy line as the whole enemy series)
Pawhaven's heroes are the toy line in `hero-style-lineup.png`: vinyl-toy / Pokopia / Animal Crossing chibi, soft matte
rounded forms, big glossy eyes with white catchlights and a thick upper lash line, blush dabs, clean colour blocking.
The approved enemies of the series so far (`approved-series-example-*.png`) show the finish. **A boss is the same toy
line, just a big, proud centrepiece figure**: the one a child remembers.

- **Cute-mischievous, never scary.** These are the bosses of a cozy game for young kids: grand, funny, a little
  pompous, never gory or menacing. Weapons and claws are toy-like: chunky, rounded.
- **Read at the game camera**: 45° down, about 20–25 m away, the boss 2.5–5 m tall. Big simple masses, a strong unique
  silhouette from above, the face visible from above, clear colour blocks.
- **Flat vertex colours only, no textures**: clean flat colour blocks with sharp borders, no painted patterns finer
  than ~5 cm, no gradients that a solid colour region couldn't carry (a pattern on a kimono must be big flat shapes).
  About 8–12 colours with hex swatches.
- **Modellable**: chunky rounded primitives and sculpted lumps, about 15–25k triangles in the end, no fussy cloth, no
  strands, no loose floating bits.
- **Moving parts**: the game animates the boss by turning a few rigid parts (no skeleton): the brief lists them. Draw
  them as **clearly separate pieces** with visible joints.
- **The current in-game model** is attached (`current-*.png`): keep its idea and its fight read; the new design should
  be clearly better crafted and more charming. **Don't copy famous designs.**

## What the sheet shows
One landscape sheet per option, light neutral background, flat even lighting, thin clean outlines:
1. **Turnaround** (front, 3/4, side, back) in the idle pose, one ground line, a 1.0 m hero silhouette for scale.
2. **Face close-up** (front and 3/4) plus **3 expressions**: angry / attacking, hurt (sweat drop), and defeated (dizzy).
3. **The signature attack poses** listed in the brief (small callouts).
4. **The forms / phase changes** listed in the brief (callouts).
5. **Hex swatches** for every colour.
Label each sheet "<BOSS> · Option A/B/C · <option name>".
