## Enemy style (the same for every monster in this series)
Pawhaven's heroes are the toy line in `hero-style-lineup.png` and `heroes-in-game.png`: vinyl-toy / Pokopia /
Animal Crossing chibi, soft matte rounded forms, big glossy eyes with white catchlights and a thick upper lash line,
blush dabs, clean colour blocking. **The monsters must look like they come from the same toy line**: collectible
little yōkai figures that sit next to the heroes on a shelf.

- **Cute-mischievous, never scary.** These are the enemies of a cozy game for young kids. Grumpy, cheeky, determined,
  sleepy, smug, never gory, sharp-toothed or menacing. Blades and claws are toy-like: chunky, rounded tips.
- **Read at the game camera**: 45° down, about 20 m away, monsters about 0.6–1.8 m tall. Big simple masses, a strong
  unique silhouette from above, the face visible from above (no brim or hood hiding the eyes from 45°), a clear
  1–3-colour body read. No thin detail that disappears at distance.
- **The 3D model will use flat vertex colours only, no textures.** So design in **clean flat colour blocks with sharp
  borders** (soft painted shading on the sheet is fine, but no painted patterns, gradients, speckles, stripes thinner
  than ~3 cm, or texture detail that a solid-colour region couldn't carry). Eyes are simple shapes: dark iris, white
  catchlight dots, lash line, a white rim if wanted. About 6–8 colours with hex swatches.
- **Modellable and light**: about 3–5k triangles in the end. Chunky rounded primitives, a few sculpted lumps, no
  fussy cloth, no strands, no loose floating bits.
- **Moving parts**: the game animates each monster by swinging a few rigid parts (no skeleton). The brief lists them.
  Design them as **clearly separate pieces** with a visible joint (a neck, a shoulder ball, a tail root), never
  fused into one blob.
- **The current in-game model** is attached (`current-*.png`). Keep its idea and its gameplay read; the new design
  should be clearly better crafted, more charming and more toy-like. It doesn't have to copy it.
- **Don't copy famous designs** (no Ghibli kodama, no Pokémon). Original takes on the folklore.

## What each sheet shows (overrides the character A-pose for non-humanoid monsters)
One landscape sheet per option, light neutral background, flat even lighting, thin clean outlines:
1. **Turnaround** in a **neutral standing / idle pose** (limbs slightly away from the body so the parts are clear):
   front, 3/4, side, back, same scale on one ground line. Add a small **1.0 m height bar** and a ghost outline of a
   1.0 m-tall hero beside the front view for scale.
2. **Face close-up** (front and 3/4) plus **3 expressions**: angry / attacking, hurt (squinting, sweat drop), and
   defeated (dizzy swirl eyes).
3. **The signature attack pose** (described per monster) as a small callout.
4. **A variant strip**: the same model in its three colourways (listed per monster), small front views side by side.
   Variants change colours and add or swap at most one small accessory, never the shape.
5. **Hex swatches** for every colour of the base colourway.
Label each sheet "<MONSTER> · Option A/B/C · <option name>".
