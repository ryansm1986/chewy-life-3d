## The character
**<Name>**: <who they are in the game (role, story, where they live), 2–3 lines>. About **1.0–1.2 m tall** in the game world.
The game now has a **Toybox Chibi** art style. Its first character is the new Chewy: the attached `sheet-B.png` (his approved
model sheet) and `chewy-toy-game.png` (him in the game). **<Name> must look like the same toy line**, as if Chewy and
<Name> were sold side by side. Deliver **3 design options**. The owner picks one, and it's then modelled in Blender.

## Style (fixed for every option: match sheet-B)
- A vinyl-toy / Pokopia / Animal Crossing feel.
  - **Proportions**: about 2.4 heads tall; a big rounded head (squircle, slightly wider than tall); a stubby, rounded
    body and limbs; mitten hands; broad rounded feet.
  - **Surfaces**: soft, matte, smooth surfaces with gentle painted shading. There's no hair-strand or fur-strand
    texture: hair and fur are a few big sculpted, smooth masses.
- The **face language of sheet-B**:
  - big glossy eyes set low and wide: a warm-white sclera showing white crescents, the iris tucked toward the nose, a
    large dark pupil, two catchlights and a thick upper lash line with a small wing;
  - soft arched brows;
  - a small rounded nose;
  - a gentle closed smile;
  - blush dabs.
- Clean colour blocking with crisp borders; a simple palette of about 5–7 colours, with hex swatches on the sheet.
- It must read from the game camera (45° down, about 20 m away): a strong silhouette, big shapes and no thin details.
