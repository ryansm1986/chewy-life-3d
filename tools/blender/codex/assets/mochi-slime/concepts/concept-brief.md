# Concept brief: mochi-slime-concept (Pawhaven enemy series, the Burrow)

The series so far: the owner approved `approved-series-example-takenoko.png` (Bamboo Grove) and
`approved-series-example-kuri.png` (Momiji Hollow). Match their style, finish and sheet layout.

The Burrow is the game's first dungeon: four little dens under the village (the Mossy Burrow, the Fox Shrine, the Oni
Kitchen, the Moon Sanctum). Its monsters are the first enemies a young player meets, so they are the simplest and
friendliest-looking of the series.

**This kind is one rigid piece** (the game bobs, squashes, tilts and spins the whole model; nothing on it moves on its
own), so design it as one solid toy figure: no separate swinging parts needed.

## The monster
**Mochi Slime**, a blob of squishy rice cake, the most common enemy in the Burrow. Packs of 6–12. About **0.6 m tall**,
0.85 m across: a soft dome.

What it does in a fight: it **bounces** after you (squashing flat, stretching tall) and bumps into you.

Identity to keep (every option): a soft round **mochi dome** (pink #ffd6e4, paler on top, a little darker at the
base where it sits), a cute grumpy face, blush, and a **topping** on top (a little red berry with a leaf). Squishy,
glossy, like a toy daifuku.

Variant strip (same model; only the colour and the topping change):
1. base: pink #ffd6e4, a strawberry topping;
2. green #d8f0c0, a **leaf** topping (a little sakura-mochi leaf);
3. **Snow Mochi**: white #fff8f4, a **snow** topping (a snowflake / snow heap), frosty blue blush.

**Boss callout (also draw it): King Mochi the Squishy**, the boss of the Mossy Burrow: the same mochi made huge and
royal (about 2.5 m tall in the game): pale pink #ffe8f0, a golden crown with jewels, a red cape with an ermine collar,
a grand curly moustache. Draw a small front + 3/4 of him beside the variant strip.

## The three options (the same style, genuinely different designs)
- **Option A, "Daifuku Drop"**: the current idea perfected: a soft round dome with a big face low on the front, the topping on top.
- **Option B, "Dango Hopper"**: a taller, bouncier teardrop-ish mochi with two little nub feet and nub arms, a cheekier face.
- **Option C, "Squish Bun"**: the most chibi: a wide, flat, extra-squishy bun with a huge face and tiny topping; the cutest.
