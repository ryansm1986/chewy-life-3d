# Asset brief: tk-fuku-toy (VL-2: the Blender Okami Fuku, phase 1: the model)

Read `../villager-toy-common.md` first: the shared technical rules, the lessons and the process rules. **Two differences
from it**:
- This is a **zone villager** (Takemori Village, the bamboo zone), not one of Blossom Hollow's seven.
- The sheet `refs/sheet.png` was drawn for this task (2026-11-02) and approved by the director. The owner delegated the
  pick. It is the blueprint: don't redesign, just build it beautifully.
`refs/current-*-portrait.png` (where present) is the procedural kit version in the game now: identity only.

## Who
**Okami Fuku**, the innkeeper of the Sasanoha Inn, a chubby tanuki: warm, bossy and motherly (her futons are legendary; so is her nagging).
The blueprint is `refs/sheet.png` ("Okami Fuku - Innkeeper").

## Must keep (from the sheet)
- **Fur** #A8846E; **muzzle and belly** #F2E2D2; the tanuki's **dark mask** #4A3A34 round the eyes (a soft band, not a raccoon bandit), dark round **ears** with pink inner #D9908A; nose #302622.
- **Eyes** amber #B8803A with the thick lash line; blush #E2948E.
- **Kimono top** coral #D8705E with a cream collar crossing; **obi** #F4D27A with a flat rounded bow at the back (back view); **apron** #FFF6E8 with rounded corners from the obi to the knees; **skirt** #6A3A3A below.
- The **big round tanuki tail**: brown with a dark band and tip, a thick soft volume (side and back views), weighted later to the tail bones.

## Sizes
- Total height **1.08 m** (the sheet's note). Villagers stand about 1.0–1.15 m to the crown.
- Measure everything else on the sheet in Step 0 (`measure.md`, fractions of the head width W). The director checks your
  table against the sheet.

## Done when
As in the shared brief:
- within about 5% of your measured targets;
- appealing in `portrait.png`;
- the outfit and accessories reading well in `game.png` from both 45° yaws;
- ≤ 30k triangles, one atlas, `joints.json`.
