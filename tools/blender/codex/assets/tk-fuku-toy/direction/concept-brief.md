# Concept brief: tk-fuku-concept (Toybox Chibi, ONE sheet)

## The character
**Okami Fuku** is a **tanuki (raccoon dog)**, the innkeeper of the Sasanoha Inn in **Takemori Village**, a cosy bamboo-forest village in a Japanese-folklore
cozy game. About **1.05 to 1.10 m, chubby** tall in the game world.

The game has a **Toybox Chibi** art style. The approved sheets show it:
- the Toybox Chewy: `sheet-B.png`, and `chewy-toy-game-crop.png` in the game;
- Rosie: `rosie-sheet-A.png`;
- **`villager-sheet-example-kuma.png`: an approved villager sheet. Match its layout, its proportions and its finish
  exactly.** Okami Fuku must look like the same toy line, as if they were sold side by side.
- `current-*-portrait.png` (if attached) is the old procedural version in the game: it shows the identity, but it's the
  look we're replacing, not the target.

Deliver **one design** on one sheet. It will be modelled in Blender by hand (not the procedural kit), so a little more
detail is welcome: soft cloth folds, a carved prop, a layered obi. Keep every shape **chunky and rounded**, with no thin
straps, fringes or dangling strings, and at most 1-2 small accessories.

## Style (fixed: match the sheets)
- A vinyl-toy / Animal Crossing feel.
- **Proportions**: about 2.4 heads tall; a big rounded head (a squircle, slightly wider than tall); a stubby, rounded body;
  mitten hands; broad, rounded feet.
- **Surfaces**: soft, matte and smooth, with gentle painted shading. There's no fur-strand texture: fur is a few big smooth
  masses.
- **The face language of these sheets**:
  - big glossy eyes **set mid-face, clearly above the muzzle**, facing a little outward: a warm-white sclera showing white
    crescents, the iris tucked toward the nose, a large dark pupil, two catchlights, and a thick upper lash line with a
    small wing;
  - soft arched brows;
  - a small rounded nose on a short toy muzzle;
  - a gentle closed smile;
  - blush dabs.
- Clean colour blocking with crisp borders; a simple palette with hex swatches.
- It must read from the game camera (45 degrees down, about 20 m away): a strong silhouette and big shapes.

## Identity (keep these)
- **Look**: warm brown fur #a8846e with a cream muzzle and belly #f2e2d2, the tanuki's dark eye mask and dark ears #4a3a34 with pink inner #d9908a, nose #302622, amber eyes #b8803a, blush #e2948e.
- **Outfit**: a coral-red kimono top #d8705e with a dark berry skirt #6a3a3a, a golden obi #f4d27a and a crisp cream apron #fff6e8; her round tanuki tail shows behind.
- **Personality**: warm, bossy, motherly; her futons are legendary and so is her nagging. Show it in the pose and the expressions.

## Sheet requirements
One landscape sheet, laid out like the Kuma sheet:
- the title "Okami Fuku - Innkeeper" top left, and the height notes top right;
- an **A-pose turnaround** in front, 3/4, side and back views, consistent with each other, on a common ground line;
- a **head close-up**, front and 3/4, with **3 expressions**: happy, talking, and hands on hips, mock-scolding;
- hex swatches with labels;
- a light, flat, evenly lit background.
Save it as `concepts/option-A.png`, plus `concepts/notes.md` with the palette and the main shapes, listed as simple volumes.

## Done when
- It reads unmistakably as Okami Fuku and as the same toy line as Chewy, Rosie and Kuma.
- The views agree well enough to model from: the same outfit, colours, ear and tail shapes in every view.
- No text other than the title, the height notes and the swatch labels. English only.
