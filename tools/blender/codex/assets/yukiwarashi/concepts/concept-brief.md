# Concept brief: yukiwarashi-concept (Pawhaven enemy series, Yukimi Onsen)

The series so far: the owner approved `approved-series-example-takenoko.png` (Bamboo Grove) and
`approved-series-example-kuri.png` (Momiji Hollow). Match their style, finish and sheet layout.

## The monster
**Yuki-warashi**, a snow child of Japanese folklore, the common ranged enemy of Yukimi Onsen (a snowy hot-spring
village). Packs of 6–12. About **1.05 m tall**.

What it does in a fight (design for this):
- It keeps its distance and **lobs volleys of snowballs** (3, or 5 for one variant): both arms go up, a snowball in each
  mitten, then it throws them one after another.
- Crowd it and it **hops back** out of reach. Between volleys it **scoops up snow** for new snowballs.

Identity to keep (every option):
- A shaggy **straw mino** raincoat in three tiers with a zig-zag fringe (#d8b262, darker #9a7434, light #f0d890; the
  inside #6a4c26), a big round **face** with a dark **okappa bob** and blunt fringe (#2c2436), rosy cheeks.
- A little **straw kasa** hat tipped back on its head with a **heap of snow** on top.
- A red knitted **scarf** (#e8403c) and big red **mittens** (#e8403c) with fluffy white cuffs on straw sleeves; little
  boots (#b88a4c).
- Personality: a mischievous, giggly snowball-fight kid.

Moving parts (draw them as separate pieces):
- the **body** (mino, head, hair, hat, scarf, boots: one rigid piece);
- the **face** features (drawn on the head, part of the body);
- the **two arms** (sleeve + mitten), each on its own shoulder joint: they swing up overhead to throw;
- a **snowball** in each mitten (separate small balls: they pop in and out).

Signature attack pose callout: the **throw**: one arm whipping forward, a snowball flying with a little arc of flakes.

Variant strip (same model, colourways):
1. base: as above;
2. **Snowdrift Warashi**: paler straw #cdb888 / #8c7a5a, hair #3a2c2a, pink mittens #ff6a98, blue scarf #4a86e8, frost on its
   mino's shoulders and a bigger snow heap on the hat;
3. **Camellia Warashi**: straw #c89a52, hair #1e1a2a, deep red mittens #d8303a, purple scarf #8a4ab8, plus a red camellia
   flower tucked in the hat.

## The three options (the same style, genuinely different designs)
- **Option A, "Straw Snow Child"**: the current idea perfected: a round little kid in a big shaggy mino bell, the face
  peeking out under the tipped kasa, big mittens.
- **Option B, "Snowball Champion"**: sportier and bouncier: a shorter mino like a cape, a big bobble-knit hat instead of
  the kasa (with the snow heap), a little pouch of snowballs at the belt, a cheeky gap-toothed grin.
- **Option C, "Snow Bun"**: the most chibi: the face is nearly the whole front of a round snow-dusted body, a tiny mino
  shawl, a tiny kasa, huge mittens; the cutest.
