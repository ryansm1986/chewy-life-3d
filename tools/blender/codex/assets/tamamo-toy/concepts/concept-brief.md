# Concept brief: tamamo-concept (Toybox Chibi, the Moon Sanctum's boss)

## The character
**Tamamo, the Nine-Tailed**: Tamamo-no-Mae, the legendary white nine-tailed fox, the final boss of the Burrow (the Moon
Sanctum, its deepest den). She walks like the heroes (rigged on the same skeleton), floats foxfire wisps round her, and
casts barrages of foxfire and lightning. The game scales her up to about **2.5 m** tall; design her as a 1.0–1.1 m
Toybox figure (2.4 heads tall) that is grand and regal. The game's Toybox Chibi style: the attached `sheet-B.png` (the
approved Chewy) and `chewy-toy-game.png`; the enemy series so far: `approved-series-example-kuri.png`. **She must look
like the same toy line**, the grandest piece of it. Deliver **3 design options**.

## Style (fixed for every option: match sheet-B)
- A vinyl-toy / Pokopia / Animal Crossing feel: a big rounded head, a stubby rounded body, mitten hands, rounded feet;
  soft matte surfaces; fur and hair as big smooth sculpted masses.
- The face language of sheet-B (big glossy eyes with catchlights and a thick lash line), here **regal and serene,
  a little smug**; her eyes rose-red (#ff3a6a irises).
- Clean colour blocking, about 6–8 colours with hex swatches. Reads at 45° from 25 m.

## Identity (every option must keep these)
- A **white kitsune** (#f8eee6, pure-white muzzle #ffffff) with tall fox ears (lavender inner #c8a8ff), blush (#ffb0d0).
- A deep **indigo kimono** (#3c2c78, darker #281a50) with gold trim (#ffd24a) and a gold obi (#ffcf3a).
- A **fox mask** worn on the side of her head and a golden **kanzashi** hairpin (regalia).
- **Nine fluffy tails** fanned out behind her like a peacock's: white, fading to a pale-blue **foxfire tip** (#9ad0ff,
  lavender #d8c8ff between). Draw them as nine big smooth plumes (the game builds the tails itself as nine copies of one
  tail: design **one tail** shape clearly in a callout, then the fan).
- Floating blue **foxfire wisps** orbit her (callout only: the game draws them).
- Personality: a serene, proud fox queen who toys with you.

## The three options (the same style, genuinely different designs)
- **Option A, "Moon Fox Queen"**: the current idea perfected: the indigo kimono, the fan of nine tails, the side mask and
  kanzashi.
- **Option B, "Shrine Maiden Kitsune"**: a miko-inspired look: a white-and-indigo hakama outfit, a gold tiara instead of
  the kanzashi, a gohei wand.
- **Option C, "Kitsune Princess"**: the most chibi: a huge head, a puffy short kimono, the tails like a giant fluffy fan;
  the cutest.

## Sheet requirements
One landscape sheet per option: an **A-pose turnaround** (front, 3/4, side, back, consistent; the tails shown fanned
behind, plus **one tail alone** as a callout), a head close-up (front and 3/4) with 2–3 expressions (smug, hurt, dizzy),
a casting pose callout, hex swatches. Lay it out like sheet-B on a light, flat background. Label
"TAMAMO · Option A/B/C · <name>".
