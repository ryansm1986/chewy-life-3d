# Concept brief: kakashi-concept (Pawhaven enemy series, Momiji Hollow)

The series so far: the owner approved `approved-series-example-takenoko.png` (Option A) and
`approved-series-example-kodama.png` (Option B) for the first region. Match their style, finish and sheet layout.

## The monster
**Kakashi**, a scarecrow yōkai, the ranged enemy of Momiji Hollow (the autumn maple region). About **1.75 m tall** (the
tallest common enemy so far: still chibi, a big head), standing on **one wooden leg** that it hops on.

What it does in a fight (design for this):
- It hops about on its one leg, arms out on the cross-bar.
- It **sends its crows**: the 2–3 little crows perched on its arms and hat **fly off** at you and come back.
- It also does a hopping body-slam.

Identity to keep (every option):
- **One wooden leg** (#8a5a3a) with a little geta base; a **straw mino skirt** (#e8c060 / #c89a40) as chunky layered
  thatch, no strands.
- A **patched kimono** (#3c4c90, an orange patch #f08a3a) on the **cross-bar arms**, with straw tufts at the sleeve ends.
- A **burlap head** (#e6d6b4) with a stitched face that the toy line makes cute: a mischievous grin and button or stitched
  eyes (the folklore "henohenomoheji" face as inspiration, but **readable, big toy eyes**, not scribbles).
- A **straw sugegasa** (#e8c060, a red band #d84a3a) pushed back so the face shows from 45° above.
- **2–3 round little crows** (#2e3048, orange beaks #f0a040, big white eyes) perched on the arms and hat.

Moving parts (separate pieces): the **body** (leg, skirt, kimono, arms, head, hat: one rigid piece) and **each crow** as
its own small piece (they take off and fly). Show the crows perched in the turnaround and add a callout of a crow in
flight (wings out).

Signature attack pose callout: **"Go, crows!"**: one arm thrust forward, crows launching with motion lines.

Variant strip (same model, colourways):
1. base: kimono #3c4c90, patch #f08a3a, hat with the red band, 2 crows;
2. **Hoshigaki Kakashi**: kimono #3f8058, patch #f4e2b0, plus a string of **dried persimmons** (#f07a2a) hanging from the
   arm;
3. **Oni Kakashi**: kimono #5a3470, patch #e8503a, **no hat**, a red-orange burlap head (#ff8a66) with two small stubby
   horns, 3 crows.

## The three options (the same style, genuinely different designs)
- **Option A, "Field Guardian"**: the current idea perfected: a classic scarecrow on its cross-bar, big round burlap head,
  wide straw hat, the kimono tied with a rope belt, crows on both arms.
- **Option B, "Puppet Prankster"**: rounder and more like a stuffed doll or a theatre puppet: a pumpkin-round head with
  big button eyes and a stitched grin, a shorter fatter skirt, the cross-bar arms ending in mitten tufts, one crow on the
  hat like a lookout.
- **Option C, "Ronin Scarecrow"**: a wandering-samurai scarecrow: a deep straw hat tilted low but the face still clear
  from above, a scarf, a little wooden toy sword at the hip, the kimono worn and patched with personality.
