  shade.
- Fatter mitts about 2 cm lower, 8-sided rounder legs, a slightly taller face plate, the crest trimmed to 0.85 m total.
- Slam: keep the upright shield (+0.6 may stop above the sand); fix the cuffs touching L1/R1 and the mitts dipping into
  the shell; walk: legs grazing the lip at the full lift.
