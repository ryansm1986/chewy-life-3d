# Concept brief: heikegani-concept (Pawhaven enemy series, Shiokaze Tidepools)

The series so far: the owner approved `approved-series-example-takenoko.png` (Bamboo Grove) and
`approved-series-example-kuri.png` (Momiji Hollow). Match their style, finish and sheet layout.

## The monster
**Heike-gani**, the samurai crab of Japanese folklore (a crab whose shell carries the face of a cross samurai), the
armoured tank of the Shiokaze Tidepools. Packs of 3–6. About **0.85 m tall** to the top of its crest and about
**1.1 m wide** with its legs out; low and broad.

What it does in a fight (design for this):
- It scuttles **sideways** in zig-zags, keeping its **two big claws held up in front like a shield**. Hits from the
  front clank off them, so you strike it from the side or behind.
- It **dashes sideways** at you when you flank it.
- The **slam**: it raises both claws high and smashes them down; they stick in the sand for a moment (guard down).

Identity to keep (every option):
- A broad, low **carapace** (#c4473a, a cream belly #f2d6b0) wider at the front, with the **samurai face on top of the
  shell** where it reads from above: heavy angry brows, glaring eyes, a big curly moustache, a frown (#2a1418 for the
  lines, #fff6e6 eye whites).
- A golden **kuwagata helmet crest** (two horns sweeping up from the back of the shell, #ffc83a) with a round clan
  **mon** badge (#e8403a) at its base.
- Two little **stalk eyes** at the front (the crab's own, cute and cross), separate from the samurai face.
- **Eight legs**, short and chunky (#b2402f, darker tips #3a1c1c), fanned fore and aft, never spidery.
- **Two big claws** lacquered like samurai gauntlets (#d44a3a, a gold cuff band and studs #ffd257, dark tips
  #3a1c1c), proper pincers with a gap.
- Personality: a grumpy, proud little warlord.

Moving parts (draw them as separate pieces):
- the **body** (carapace, face, crest, mon and stalk eyes: one rigid piece);
- **eight legs**, each its own piece hinged at the hip on the shell's side (they step);
- the **claws**, as one part (both pitch up and down together from the shoulders).

Signature attack pose callout: the **slam**: both claws raised high over the shell, then the smash with a sand puff.

Variant strip (same model, colourways):
1. base: as above;
2. **Genji-gani** (the rival clan, white): shell #aebfd6, claws #c4d2e6, legs #8c9cb8, face lines #232a5a, crest
   #e2e8f2, mon #3a5ab8, trim #e8f0ff;
3. **Shogun-gani** (black lacquer): shell #3e3548, claws #352c40, legs #4a3c54, face lines and trim gold #ffcf4a, crest
   #ff5a3a, claw tips #ff5a3a.

## The three options (the same style, genuinely different designs)
- **Option A, "Samurai Shell"**: the current idea perfected: a broad shield-shaped shell with a big expressive samurai
  face, a tall golden crest, chunky gauntlet claws held up like a guard.
- **Option B, "Armoured General"**: the shell itself styled like samurai armour: a kabuto-like dome with flared
  lacquered side plates (like a helmet's neck guard) and lacing bands, the face like a fierce menpo mask on top, the
  claws like armoured gauntlets with big round knuckle plates.
- **Option C, "Grumpy Bun"**: the most chibi: a rounder, higher dome like a bun, a softer pouty samurai face, a smaller
  crest, oversized mitten-like claws, tiny round legs; the cutest.
