# Concept brief: danzaburo-concept (Pawhaven boss, Momiji Hollow)

The enemy series so far: `approved-series-example-takenoko.png` and `approved-series-example-kuri.png` show the approved
style; `approved-region-in-game.jpg` shows this region's approved Blender enemies in the game (the AFTER half), the
ones this boss leads. Match their style and finish; the boss is the grand centrepiece of the set.

## The boss
**Danzaburō the Leaf-Shifter**, the great tanuki trickster of folklore, the boss of Momiji Hollow (and its dungeon's
hall). About **2.6 m** tall in the game, very round; in phase 3 he grows into a giant.

What he does in the fight (design for this):
- **Belly drum**: he drums his big cream belly ("PON! PON!"): shock rings roll out.
- **Doron! Boulder**: he slaps the leaf on his head and turns into a **mossy boulder** (his ears, tail and eyes still
  peeking out) that rolls at you.
- **Doron! Kettle**: he turns into a **bunbuku chagama** (a tea kettle with his head, legs and tail), hops closer, the lid
  rattles and the spout spews steam.
- **Belly bump**, and he whistles on his leaf for his tanuki gang.
- Phase 3: he chugs his sake, goes red in the cheeks and grows into a giant.

Identity to keep (every option):
- A great round **tanuki**: brown fur, a drum of a **cream belly**, dark "bandit" **eye patches**, round ears.
- A straw **sugegasa** hat pushed back with a bright green **transformation leaf** on top.
- A **sake tokkuri** (a ceramic flask with a straw-rope tie) in one paw, a huge **ringed tail**.
- Personality: a jolly, tipsy, show-off trickster with a big grin.

Moving parts (separate pieces): the body; the **belly** (its own piece: it wobbles as he drums); the **head** (with an
open **jaw** for the laugh, and **blush** cheeks for the tipsy phase); the hat with the leaf; the two **arms** (one with the
tokkuri); the **tail**. Plus the two **forms** as their own models: the **boulder** (mossy rock with the leaf, ears, eyes and
tail peeking out) and the **kettle** (bunbuku chagama: a lidded kettle with a separate **lid**, his head peeking out the
front, the spout, little legs and the tail).

Signature poses (callouts): the **belly drum**; the **boulder** form rolling; the **kettle** form whistling steam; the
**giant** phase (red cheeks, sake raised).

## The three options (the same style, genuinely different designs)
- **Option A, "Drum Belly"**: the current idea perfected: a huge round tanuki, the drum belly front and centre, the hat pushed back, the tokkuri and the ringed tail.
- **Option B, "Leaf Showman"**: a showman trickster: a short haori jacket open over the belly, a fan of leaves tucked in the belt, a bigger theatrical grin.
- **Option C, "Mochi Tanuki"**: the most chibi: nearly a perfect ball, tiny legs, the belly the whole front, a tiny hat; the cutest.
