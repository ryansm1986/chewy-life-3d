# Concept brief: iwabozu-concept (Pawhaven enemy series, Bamboo Depths)

The series so far: the owner approved `approved-series-example-takenoko.png` (Bamboo Grove) and
`approved-series-example-kuri.png` (Momiji Hollow). Match their style, finish and sheet layout.

## The monster
**Iwa-bōzu**, a rock monk, the slow armoured **tank** of Bamboo Depths (the zone dungeon under the Bamboo Grove). Packs of
1–2 among the grove's quick yōkai. About **1.2 m tall**, a round boulder about 1.1 m across.

What it does in a fight (design for this):
- It **dozes looking like any other boulder** (eyes shut, a slow breath) until someone comes close: it **wakes** (a hop,
  eyes pop open), then trundles after you.
- **Slam** (close): both stone arms up, a ground thump.
- **Roll** (mid range): it tucks into a ball (arms pulled in) and barrels along a lane, then sits dizzy.

Identity to keep (every option):
- A round, mossy **boulder** body (stone #a0968a, light #cbbfae, darker underneath) with a **moss cap** on top (#6a9a3c,
  light #a6c860) and **two little takenoko shoots** sprouting from the moss.
- A sleepy, kindly monk **face** in the stone: heavy lids, a calm little mouth; awake: round surprised eyes.
- A straw **shimenawa** rope belt hung with white zig-zag paper **shide**.
- **Stubby stone arms** and stone feet (#6e665c).
- Personality: a sleepy, gentle giant who's grumpy to be woken.

Moving parts (draw them as separate pieces):
- the **body** (boulder, face, moss cap, shoots, rope belt, feet: one rigid piece);
- the **eyelids** (a separate closed-eyes piece over the eyes, shown while it sleeps: draw the face awake AND asleep);
- the **two arms**, each on its own shoulder joint (raised overhead for the slam; tucked in when rolling).

Signature attack pose callout: the **roll**: tucked into a ball, tumbling, moss flying.

Variant strip (same model, colourways):
1. base: as above;
2. **Lantern Iwa-bōzu**: stone #a89c8e, a tiny glowing **stone lantern** sitting in its moss cap;
3. **Elder Iwa-bōzu**: stone #968e86, a long **white lichen beard** and a bigger moss cap.

## The three options (the same style, genuinely different designs)
- **Option A, "Mossy Monk"**: the current idea perfected: a round boulder monk, the moss cap like a monk's cap, the
  shimenawa belt, stubby arms.
- **Option B, "Stone Jizō"**: inspired by the little roadside Jizō statues: a taller, egg-shaped stone body, a red bib
  (yodarekake), hands that press together in prayer, a serene face.
- **Option C, "Pebble Bub"**: the most chibi: a very round squat pebble, a face nearly the whole front, tiny arms, a
  floppy moss cap; the cutest.
