# Asset brief: tk-kome-toy (VL-2: the Blender Kome, phase 1: the model)

Read `../villager-toy-common.md` first: the shared technical rules, the lessons and the process rules. **Two differences
from it**:
- This is a **zone villager** (Takemori Village, the bamboo zone), not one of Blossom Hollow's seven.
- The sheet `refs/sheet.png` was drawn for this task (2026-11-02) and approved by the director. The owner delegated the
  pick. It is the blueprint: don't redesign, just build it beautifully.
`refs/current-*-portrait.png` (where present) is the procedural kit version in the game now: identity only.

## Who
**Kome**, the Sasanoha Inn's cook, a sweet, bubbly calico cat whose bamboo-shoot rice is so good the yokai kidnapped her for it.
The blueprint is `refs/sheet.png` ("Kome - Cook").

## Must keep (from the sheet)
- **Fur** cream #FFF2E4 with **orange calico patches** #E89A5A: **use the FRONT view** (a patch over her left eye and ear, the viewer's right; the side view disagrees, ignore it), one patch on the back (back view), and the tail orange with a cream tip.
- **Ears** orange with pink inner #FFB0C0; nose #CB7F86; **green eyes** #5AA86A; blush #F5A0AD.
- A **white cook's headband** #FFFFFF, a thick band.
- **Kimono top** #F2B8C8; **white obi** #FFFFFF; **apron** #FFF8EE; **plum skirt** #6A4A5A.
- Her **wooden rice paddle** (shamoji, #B8834F) tucked in the obi at her right hip, a chunky rounded piece.

## Sizes
- Total height **1.03 m** (the sheet's note). Villagers stand about 1.0–1.15 m to the crown.
- Measure everything else on the sheet in Step 0 (`measure.md`, fractions of the head width W). The director checks your
  table against the sheet.

## Done when
As in the shared brief:
- within about 5% of your measured targets;
- appealing in `portrait.png`;
- the outfit and accessories reading well in `game.png` from both 45° yaws;
- ≤ 30k triangles, one atlas, `joints.json`.
