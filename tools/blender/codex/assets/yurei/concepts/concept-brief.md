# Concept brief: yurei-concept (Pawhaven enemy series, the Haunted modifier)

The series so far: the owner approved `approved-series-example-takenoko.png` (Bamboo Grove) and
`approved-series-example-kuri.png` (Momiji Hollow). Match their style, finish and sheet layout.

## The monster
**Yūrei**, a little sheet ghost that rises from a fallen monster in a **Haunted** dungeon run (any zone). Swarms of
fragile, quick fodder. About **0.8 m tall**, floating a hand (0.3 m) above the floor.

What it does in a fight (design for this):
- It **rises out of a fallen monster** (a swirl of motes, a sigh) and drifts after you.
- Close up it **boos**: it rears back, then lunges with a chilling "boo!".

Identity to keep (every option):
- A soft white **sheet** with a scalloped **hem** (sheet #e4e2ef, shade #b0b8de, hem #8e9cd2).
- Big round **eyes**, a small "oh" mouth, pink cheeks.
- The white paper triangle **hitaikakushi** of the departed on its brow.
- Two drooping **sleeves** held out in front in the old "urameshiya" pose.
- A blue **hitodama** spirit flame circling it (the game makes it glow).
- Personality: a timid, sad-sweet little ghost trying very hard to be scary.

Moving parts (draw them as separate pieces):
- the **body** (sheet, face, brow triangle: one rigid piece);
- the **two arms/sleeves**, each on its own shoulder joint;
- the **spirit flame** (a separate small teardrop flame that circles the ghost).

Signature attack pose callout: the **boo**: reared back then lunging, sleeves flung up, mouth wide.

Variant strip (same model, colourways; this kind has two):
1. base: as above;
2. **Sleepy Yūrei**: a floppy blue **nightcap** with a pompom (#6a8ee0), sleepy half-closed eyes.

## The three options (the same style, genuinely different designs)
- **Option A, "Sheet Ghost"**: the current idea perfected: a soft draped sheet with a scalloped hem, the brow triangle,
  sleeves held out.
- **Option B, "Lantern Yūrei"**: a ghost in a little white kimono with long sleeves and a wavy wisp of a tail instead of
  a hem, carrying its flame in a tiny lantern on a pole.
- **Option C, "Mochi Ghost"**: the most chibi: a round, squishy little ghost like a mochi with a tiny hem, a huge face,
  stubby sleeves; the cutest.
