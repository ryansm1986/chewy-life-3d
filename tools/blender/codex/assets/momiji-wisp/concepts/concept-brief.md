# Concept brief: momijiWisp-concept (Pawhaven enemy series, Momiji Hollow)

The series so far: the owner approved `approved-series-example-takenoko.png` (Option A) and
`approved-series-example-kodama.png` (Option B) for the first region. Match their style, finish and sheet layout.

## The monster
**Momiji Wisp**, a little autumn-leaf spirit, the floating caster of Momiji Hollow (the autumn maple region). About
**1.0 m** across its leaf mane, the glowing core about 0.4 m, and it **floats** about 0.55 m off the ground.

What it does in a fight (design for this):
- It drifts and blinks (teleports) about, and flings **fans of maple-leaf darts**.
- Its tell: the **ring of orbiting leaves** round it spins up faster and tightens, then flings out.

Identity to keep (every option):
- A **glowing warm core** (#ffe07a with a deeper #ffb050) with the face on it: a lantern-like glow is its signature.
- A **mane of maple leaves** (#e2482e / #f47034) fanned behind the core, with **two leaf "ears"** on top.
- A **spiral wisp-tail** below with little leaves.
- A **ring of 5–7 orbiting maple leaves** around it (a separate piece).
- Personality: playful, giggly, a little trickster (current look: big eyes, rosy cheeks).
- Maple leaves are **chunky toy shapes** (5–7 rounded points, ≥ 3 cm thick), not paper-thin.

Moving parts (separate pieces): the **body** (core, face, mane, ears, tail: one rigid piece) and the **orbit ring** of
leaves (spins about the vertical axis, tightens and flings out). Show the ring in the turnaround as a loose circle round it.

Signature attack pose callout: the **whirl**: the ring spun into a tight blurred circle, the mane flared, about to fling.

Variant strip (same model, colourways):
1. base: maple leaves #e2482e / #f47034, core #ffe07a / #ffb050;
2. **Ginkgo Wisp**: the leaves as **fan-shaped ginkgo leaves** (#ffd23a / #eeb028), core #fff2b8 / #ffd070;
3. **Ember Wisp** (fire): leaves #b81e2a / #ff6a2a, core #ffb24a / #ff6a2a, little ember sparks in the mane.

## The three options (the same style, genuinely different designs)
- **Option A, "Leaf Lantern"**: the current idea perfected: a round glowing core-face like a lantern, a full sunburst
  mane of maple leaves behind it, two leaf ears, a curly wisp tail.
- **Option B, "Leaf Kit"**: a little **fox-spirit** read: the core a round fox-like face with the two leaf ears as pointed
  fox ears, the mane like a ruff, the tail a big curled leafy fox tail (still a floating wisp, no legs).
- **Option C, "Momiji Sprite"**: a tiny floating **fairy-child** made of leaves: a small glowing body with stubby leaf
  arms, a big leaf hood framing the face (face visible from above), the mane as leaf wings behind it.
