# Concept brief: kinoko-concept (Pawhaven enemy series, the Burrow)

The series so far: the owner approved `approved-series-example-takenoko.png` (Bamboo Grove) and
`approved-series-example-kuri.png` (Momiji Hollow). Match their style, finish and sheet layout.

The Burrow is the game's first dungeon: four little dens under the village (the Mossy Burrow, the Fox Shrine, the Oni
Kitchen, the Moon Sanctum). Its monsters are the first enemies a young player meets, so they are the simplest and
friendliest-looking of the series.

**This kind is one rigid piece** (the game bobs, squashes, tilts and spins the whole model; nothing on it moves on its
own), so design it as one solid toy figure: no separate swinging parts needed.

## The monster
**Kinoko**, a little mushroom yōkai, the slow spore-caster of the Burrow. Packs of 2–6. About **0.75 m tall**
(the game scales it ×0.8).

What it does in a fight: it **waddles** about, then **puffs a cloud of spores** round itself (a stinky area attack).

Identity to keep (every option): a big round **mushroom cap** (red #ff5a5a with cream-white spots), a short pale
**stem** body (#f6ecd8) with a face, tiny feet. Toy-like, chunky.

Variant strip (same model, colourways): 1. red #ff5a5a with white spots; 2. **Dream Kinoko**: purple #8a6aff with pale
lilac spots; 3. orange #ffae3a with cream spots.

## The three options (the same style, genuinely different designs)
- **Option A, "Toadstool Tot"**: the current idea perfected: a big spotted cap, a short stem with the face under the cap's rim.
- **Option B, "Kasa Kinoko"**: a mushroom wearing its cap like a wide straw hat, little arms, a sleepy-grumpy face.
- **Option C, "Button Bub"**: the most chibi: a huge round cap and a tiny stem, the face on the cap's underside; the cutest.
