# Model brief: mochi-king (enemy series EN-1, the Burrow's first boss)

The same contract and one-piece note as `brief.md` (a single `body` object, origin at the ground centre, vertex colours
only, `toon` material). Build **King Mochi the Squishy** from the **callout on the approved sheet** (`refs/sheet.png`,
bottom right: front and 3/4), on top of your approved mochi slime (same dome, same face style).

## What it is
The boss of the Mossy Burrow (the game's very first boss, a young player's first one): a huge, very serious royal mochi.
He bounces, slams (a big squash) and summons little mochi. The game scales the model **×3.0** (about **2.5 m** tall in
the game), so build it about **0.83 m** tall to the top of the crown, the dome about 0.95 m across. One rigid piece: the
game bobs, squashes and tilts it whole.

## Look (follow the sheet's callout)
- The dome: pale pink **#ffe8f0**, the lighter top and the darker base band as on your slime.
- A **golden crown** (#f2c24a, darker #c8952a) with rounded points and **jewels** (red #d94351 in front, blue and green at
  the sides), sitting on a red **velvet cap** (#c8283a) on top.
- A **red royal cape** (#c8283a, inside darker) falling from the "shoulders" behind him to the floor, with a white
  **ermine collar** (#fbf8f4 with little dark tail-tip dots #2a2430) round the front. The cape is a closed shell with a
  thickness (≥ 3 cm), not a single sheet.
- A grand **curly moustache** (#6a3e2a, two plump lobes with up-curled tips) under a very serious face (stern brows,
  big eyes with catchlights, blush).
- Chunky and toy-like: no thin points (the crown's points and the moustache tips are rounded).

## Budget and file
≤ 4,500 triangles. One GLB: `D:/projects/chewy-life-3d/public/models/mochi-king.glb`.

## Done when
Matches the callout (front and 3/4); reads at the 45° game camera at 10 m and 20 m (at the boss's ×3 size) with the
crown, moustache and cape clear; one `body` object.
