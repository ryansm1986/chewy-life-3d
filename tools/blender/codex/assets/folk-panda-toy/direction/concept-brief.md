# Concept brief: folk-panda-concept (Toybox Chibi, ONE sheet, THREE townsfolk)

## The characters
Three different **pandas** who move into **Blossom Hollow**, a cozy Japanese-folklore village, as it grows: ordinary
friendly townsfolk (a gardener, a baker's helper, a schoolkid, a fisher, a retired grandpa, a dreamer...). Each is about
**1.05 m** tall in the game world (a child-like one may be smaller, a chubby one rounder). Make the three **genuinely
different characters**: different fur colours from the palette, different builds, outfits and personalities. 

- **Fur palette** (the game's): #fbf8f4 white with near-black eye patches, ears and limbs.
- **Outfit ideas** (the game's): shirts, kimono tops, work overalls, a martial-arts gi, dresses, or just fur with one signature accessory (a scarf, a shoulder bag); hats: a straw hat, a beret, a flower by one ear, a bandana or a leaf on the head. One coherent outfit each, in soft cheerful colours.

The game has a **Toybox Chibi** art style. The approved sheets show it:
- the Toybox Chewy: `sheet-B.png`, and `chewy-toy-game-crop.png` in the game;
- Rosie: `rosie-sheet-A.png`;
- **`villager-sheet-example-kuma.png`: an approved villager sheet. Match its proportions and finish exactly.** These
  townsfolk must look like the same toy line, as if sold side by side.

Each design will be modelled in Blender by hand, so keep every shape **chunky and rounded**: no thin straps, fringes or
dangling strings, at most 1-2 small accessories, any strap a thick rounded band.

## Style (fixed: match the sheets)
- A vinyl-toy / Animal Crossing feel; about 2.4 heads tall; a big rounded head (a squircle, slightly wider than tall); a
  stubby rounded body; mitten hands; broad rounded feet.
- Soft, matte, smooth surfaces with gentle painted shading; no fur-strand texture.
- The face language: big glossy eyes set mid-face, clearly above the muzzle, facing a little outward (warm-white sclera,
  the iris tucked toward the nose, a large dark pupil, two catchlights, a thick upper lash line with a small wing); soft
  arched brows; a small rounded nose on a short toy muzzle; a gentle closed smile; blush dabs.
- Clean colour blocking with crisp borders. It must read from the game camera (45 degrees down, about 20 m away).

## Sheet requirements
One landscape sheet: the title "Townsfolk: pandas" top left; for each of the three designs (labelled A, B, C), a **front
and a 3/4 view** on a common ground line, a **side and a back view** (smaller is fine), a **head close-up** (front), and
hex swatches. A light, flat, evenly lit background. Save it as `concepts/option-A.png` (the sheet with all three), plus
`concepts/notes.md` with each design's palette, outfit and main shapes as simple volumes.

## Done when
- Three clearly different, charming pandas, each unmistakably the same toy line as Chewy, Rosie and Kuma.
- Each design's views agree well enough to model from.
- No text other than the title, the A / B / C labels and the swatch labels. English only (no Japanese text).
