# Concept brief: takenoko-concept (Pawhaven enemy series, Whispering Bamboo Grove)

## The monster
**Takenoko**, a bamboo-shoot sprite, the most common fodder enemy of the Whispering Bamboo Grove (the first wild
region). Packs of 8–16. About **1.1 m tall** including its curled tip, about 0.8 m wide; a plump, bottom-heavy shoot.

What it does in a fight (design for this):
- It **burrows**: only a **dirt mound with its green tip peeking out** slides along the ground towards you.
- It **pops up** under you with a **spin-slash** (twirling with its leaf arms out like little blades).
- Then it stays up and fights, waddling on root-nub toes, until it digs again.

Identity to keep (every option):
- A bamboo shoot wrapped in **pointed husk sheaths** (brown #a8743c with darker #6a4424 markings) that curl out at the
  tips, layered like an artichoke or a helmet.
- A **cream belly or face patch** (#fff0c8) where the face sits, below the husks: the face must be visible from 45°
  above, so the husks never cover the eyes.
- A **curled green tip** on top (#b8cc5a), the signature from above and the only part showing when it burrows.
- **Leaf-blade arms** (#9ac050), stubby **root-nub toes** (#ecd6a8).
- Personality: a determined, slightly grumpy little sprout with a frown (current look: furrowed brows, blush).

Moving parts (draw them as separate pieces): the **body** (one rigid piece: husks, face, toes), the two **leaf arms**
(attached at the sides at a shoulder point; they spin out for the slash), and the **dirt mound** (a separate prop: a
0.9 m wide, 0.25 m tall lumpy soil mound, #7a5a38 with lighter clods, with a hole the green tip pokes out of; show
it as its own callout).

Signature attack pose callout: the **pop-up spin-slash**: half out of the mound, arms flung out, a little leaf swirl.

Variant strip (same model, colourways):
1. base: husk #a8743c / #6a4424, tip #b8cc5a, cream #fff0c8, arms #9ac050;
2. **Aotake Shoot** (young green bamboo): husk #86b04a / #557a2a, tip #dcec7a, cream #f8f6d4, arms #b8dc6a;
3. **Golden Takenoko** (rare): husk #e0a830 / #a8601c, tip #ff7a4a, cream #fff6d8, arms #ffc04a, plus three tiny
   sparkle gems set in the husk.

## The three options (the same style, genuinely different designs)
- **Option A, "Husk Helmet"**: the current idea perfected. Round and squat (about 1 : 1.3 width to height), the
  husks layered like a pointed samurai helmet over a wide cream face; chunky leaf arms like short swords; the tip a
  tight curl.
- **Option B, "Shoot Ninja"**: taller and slimmer (1 : 1.8), the top husk folded forward into a little hood or cowl
  that frames the face (eyes still clear from above), husk sheaths wrapped like a ninja's leg and arm wraps, slender
  leaf-blade arms, an energetic springy stance.
- **Option C, "Sprout Bub"**: the most chibi: a big round bulb that's mostly face, a short tuft of three husk leaves
  on top like a topknot and a long curly tip, tiny root feet, one arm much bigger (a broad leaf it swings like a
  fan-sword). Rounder, softer and more toddler-like than A.
