# Concept brief: kasa-concept (Pawhaven enemy series, the Burrow)

The series so far: the owner approved `approved-series-example-takenoko.png` (Bamboo Grove) and
`approved-series-example-kuri.png` (Momiji Hollow). Match their style, finish and sheet layout.

The Burrow is the game's first dungeon: four little dens under the village (the Mossy Burrow, the Fox Shrine, the Oni
Kitchen, the Moon Sanctum). Its monsters are the first enemies a young player meets, so they are the simplest and
friendliest-looking of the series.

**This kind is one rigid piece** (the game bobs, squashes, tilts and spins the whole model; nothing on it moves on its
own), so design it as one solid toy figure: no separate swinging parts needed.

## The monster
**Kasa-obake**, a haunted paper umbrella yōkai, the hopping charger of the Burrow. Packs of 2–6. About **1.6 m tall**.

What it does in a fight: it **hops** on its one leg, then **charges** at you in a dash.

Identity to keep (every option): a closed-ish **paper umbrella** canopy with pleats (purple #8a5ad0) and a wooden tip,
**one big eye**, a long **tongue** lolling out (pink), and **one leg** (the umbrella's handle) ending in a geta sandal.

Variant strip (same model, colourways): 1. purple #8a5ad0; 2. red #e8503a; 3. blue #3a8ad0.

**Boss callout (also draw it): Lord Karakasa**, the boss of the Fox Shrine: the same umbrella made huge and lordly
(about 4.3 m tall): crimson #c8364a, a second skirt tier of canopy, tassels round the rim, a little crown and an ofuda
charm, an extra-long tongue. Draw a small front + 3/4 of him beside the variant strip.

## The three options (the same style, genuinely different designs)
- **Option A, "One-Eyed Umbrella"**: the current idea perfected: a pleated canopy, one big eye, the tongue, one geta leg.
- **Option B, "Open Parasol"**: a half-open wagasa like a skirt with a face under the rim, two eyes, the handle leg.
- **Option C, "Brolly Bub"**: the most chibi: a short fat umbrella that's nearly all eye, a stubby leg; the cutest.
