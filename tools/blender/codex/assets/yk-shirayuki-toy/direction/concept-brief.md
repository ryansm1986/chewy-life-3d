# Concept brief: yk-shirayuki-concept (Toybox Chibi, ONE sheet)

## The character
**Granny Shirayuki** is a **bunny**, the elder in **Yukimi Spa Village, a snowy hot-spring village of thatched roofs, steam and lanterns on the snow**, in a Japanese-folklore cozy game. Has counted every snowfall since she was a kit. She is at 61,212. **about 0.95 m** tall in the
game world.

The game has a **Toybox Chibi** art style. The approved sheets show it:
- the Toybox Chewy: `sheet-B.png`, and `chewy-toy-game-crop.png` in the game;
- Rosie: `rosie-sheet-A.png`;
- **`villager-sheet-example-kuma.png`: an approved villager sheet. Match its layout, its proportions and its finish
  exactly.** Granny Shirayuki must look like the same toy line, as if they were sold side by side.
- `current-*-portrait.png` (if attached) is the old procedural version in the game: it shows the identity, but it's the
  look we're replacing, not the target.

Deliver **one design** on one sheet. It will be modelled in Blender by hand, so a little more detail is welcome (soft
cloth folds, a carved prop), but keep every shape **chunky and rounded**: no thin straps, fringes or dangling strings, at
most 1-2 small accessories, and any strap a thick rounded band.

## Style (fixed: match the sheets)
- A vinyl-toy / Animal Crossing feel.
- **Proportions**: about 2.4 heads tall; a big rounded head (a squircle, slightly wider than tall); a stubby, rounded body;
  mitten hands; broad, rounded feet.
- **Surfaces**: soft, matte and smooth, with gentle painted shading. There's no fur-strand texture.
- **The face language of these sheets**:
  - big glossy eyes set mid-face, clearly above the muzzle, facing a little outward: a warm-white sclera, the iris
    tucked toward the nose, a large dark pupil, two catchlights, and a thick upper lash line with a small wing;
  - soft arched brows;
  - a small rounded nose on a short toy muzzle;
  - a gentle closed smile;
  - blush dabs.
- Clean colour blocking with crisp borders; a simple palette with hex swatches.
- It must read from the game camera (45 degrees down, about 20 m away): a strong silhouette and big shapes.

## Identity (keep these)
- **Look**: fur #f6f4f8; a lighter muzzle and belly #ffffff; ears #f0eef4 with inner #f0c0d0; nose #e8a0b0; eyes #7a6aa0; blush #f0b0c0; small and young or slight.
- **Outfit**: a kimono top #5a6aa8; a skirt or trousers #2e3460; a sash or obi #e8e0f0; a kerchief #d84a5a. Interpret these as one coherent, charming outfit fitting the village.
- **Personality**: Has counted every snowfall since she was a kit. She is at 61,212. Show it in the pose and the expressions.

## Sheet requirements
One landscape sheet, laid out like the Kuma sheet:
- the title "Granny Shirayuki - Elder" top left, and the height notes top right;
- an **A-pose turnaround** in front, 3/4, side and back views, consistent with each other, on a common ground line;
- a **head close-up**, front and 3/4, with **3 expressions**: happy, talking, and a fond, knowing smile with eyes closed;
- hex swatches with labels;
- a light, flat, evenly lit background.
Save it as `concepts/option-A.png`, plus `concepts/notes.md` with the palette and the main shapes, listed as simple volumes.

## Done when
- It reads unmistakably as Granny Shirayuki and as the same toy line as Chewy, Rosie and Kuma.
- The views agree well enough to model from: the same outfit, colours, ear and tail shapes, and markings in every view.
- No text other than the title, the height notes and the swatch labels. English only.
