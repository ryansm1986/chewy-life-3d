# Concept brief: sazae-oni-concept (Pawhaven enemy series, Tide Caves)

The series so far: the owner approved `approved-series-example-takenoko.png` (Bamboo Grove) and
`approved-series-example-kuri.png` (Momiji Hollow). Match their style, finish and sheet layout.

## The monster
**Sazae-oni**, a turban snail grown old enough to become an oni, the slow armoured **tank** of Tide Caves (the zone
dungeon under the Shiokaze Tidepools). Packs of 1–2. About **1.15 m tall**, the spiral shell about 1 m long.

What it does in a fight (design for this):
- It **sits in its shell with the lid shut** like any other shell on the sand until someone comes close: then it pops
  out and glides after you on its snail foot.
- **Spine star** (mid range): it shuts itself in, its **spines bristle**, and it fires them out in a star.
- **Shell spin** (close): it shuts itself in and **spins like a top**, spines out; afterwards it is dizzy and its head
  lolls out. While shut in it takes half damage.

Identity to keep (every option):
- A big spiky **turban shell** spiral (#5e7a66 with a cream band #c8c09a; spines #6e8270 with cream tips #ece4cc), a
  pearly mouth.
- A frilly **snail foot** (#8a7e72, a lighter frill edge #c8a898) gliding on the sand.
- Peeking out of the shell's mouth: a round red **oni face** (#e8705e) with two ivory **horns** (#f6eedc), a seaweed tuft
  of hair (#4a8a4a), a fangy grin (toy-like rounded fangs), stubby arms.
- A round **operculum lid** (#d8c4a4) that closes the mouth when it hides.
- Personality: a crabby old sea oni, grumpy and stubborn.

Moving parts (draw them as separate pieces):
- the **body** (the shell and the foot: one rigid piece);
- the **spines** (the shell's spikes as one separate piece: they bristle out);
- the **head** (the oni face, horns, hair and arms), its pivot inside the shell's mouth: it pops out and pulls back in;
- the **lid** (the operculum: shown when it is shut in).

Signature attack pose callout: the **shell spin**: shut in, spinning like a top, spines out, sand spraying.

Variant strip (same model, colourways):
1. base: as above;
2. **Hotaru Sazae-oni**: a blue shell #34548a with band #7ab8d8, blue skin #6aa0d8, the shell **spotted with glowing
   plankton** (cyan #bff4ff dots);
3. **Elder Sazae-oni**: a mauve shell #7a6a80, barnacles and hanging weed on the shell, green skin #7ab070, a **white beard**.

## The three options (the same style, genuinely different designs)
- **Option A, "Turban Oni"**: the current idea perfected: a tall spiky turban shell, the round oni face peeking from the
  mouth, the snail foot.
- **Option B, "Shell Samurai"**: the shell like a great armoured helmet-house, the oni face with a fierce moustache, the
  spines like spear tips, the lid like a round shield.
- **Option C, "Snail Bub"**: the most chibi: a round, squat shell like a cinnamon bun with chunky spines, a big face with
  stubby horns peeking out; the cutest.
