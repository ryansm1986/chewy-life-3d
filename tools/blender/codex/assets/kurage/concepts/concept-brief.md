# Concept brief: kurage-concept (Pawhaven enemy series, Shiokaze Tidepools)

The series so far: the owner approved `approved-series-example-takenoko.png` (Bamboo Grove) and
`approved-series-example-kuri.png` (Momiji Hollow). Match their style, finish and sheet layout.

## The monster
**Kurage**, a lantern jellyfish (chōchin kurage), the zapping caster of the Shiokaze Tidepools. Packs of 4–8. It
**floats** about 1 m off the ground: the bell is about **0.85 m across and 0.45 m tall**, the tentacles hang about
0.8 m below it, about **1.6 m** from the cap to the tentacle tips.

What it does in a fight (design for this):
- It floats and bobs, its bell pulsing, its tentacles swaying and trailing behind as it drifts in close.
- It **charges up**: the glowing core inside flares, sparks crackle round it.
- Then it sends out **expanding electric rings** across the ground (zap).

Identity to keep (every option):
- A **translucent bell** shaped like a Japanese paper lantern (#ffbf78), with lantern ribs and a few seams. In the
  game the bell is see-through (about 40 % opaque, brighter at the rim), so **design the inside too**: a glowing
  **lantern-flame core** (#fff0a0) visible through the bell.
- A dark lacquer **cap** with a little carry loop on top and a lacquer **rim band** round the bell's edge (#4a2630),
  like a real chōchin.
- A sleepy, sweet **face** on the front of the bell (it must still read through/over the translucency: draw it as a
  solid face on the bell's surface), blush.
- A **frill** of pastel lobes under the rim (#ff9a68), **four frilly oral arms** (#ffd4b4) hanging from the centre and
  **trailing tentacles** (#ffe6cc) with **glowing tips** (core colour). Tentacles must be chunky soft ribbons or
  beaded strands, not hair-thin lines (they must read at 20 m).
- Personality: sleepy, dreamy, a bit spaced-out, until it zaps.

Moving parts (draw them as separate pieces):
- the **bell** (translucent, its own piece) with the **core** inside it (glowing, its own piece);
- the **cap**, rim band, face and frill (one rigid piece with the bell);
- the **oral arms** and the **tentacles** (they sway, trail and pulse).

Signature attack pose callout: the **zap**: the core flaring white-hot, the bell squeezed, a bright ring rippling out
across the ground.

Variant strip (same model, colourways):
1. base, **Chōchin Kurage**: as above;
2. **Ao Kurage** (blue): bell #86d0ff, core #e6fcff, frill #6aaeff, arms #cdeeff, tentacles #e8f8ff, cap #233a5e;
3. **Sakura Kurage** (pink): bell #ffa6d2, core #fff2fa, frill #ff78b6, arms #ffd2ea, tentacles #ffe8f6, cap #5a2448,
   plus a little sakura blossom on its cap.

## The three options (the same style, genuinely different designs)
- **Option A, "Paper Lantern"**: the current idea perfected: a ribbed chōchin bell with the flame glowing inside, a
  neat lacquer cap and rim, a frill skirt, long trailing tentacles.
- **Option B, "Wind Chime"**: a fūrin-style bell: rounder, like a glass summer wind chime, the core hanging inside
  like the chime's clapper, a single chunky paper tanzaku-like ribbon tentacle in the middle among shorter ones.
- **Option C, "Mochi Jelly"**: the most chibi: a squishy round daifuku-like bell, a big face, a few short chunky
  tentacles like little legs, the core a round glowing heart; the cutest.
