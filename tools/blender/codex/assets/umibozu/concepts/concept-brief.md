# Concept brief: umibozu-concept (Pawhaven boss, the Shiokaze Tidepools)

The enemy series so far: `approved-series-example-takenoko.png` and `approved-series-example-kuri.png` show the approved
style; `approved-region-in-game.jpg` shows this region's approved Blender enemies in the game (the AFTER half), the
ones this boss leads. Match their style and finish; the boss is the grand centrepiece of the set.

## The boss
**Umibōzu**, the sleepy sea-monk of folklore: a huge, round sea spirit seen from the chest up, rising out of the surf at
the arena's sea edge. About **5 m** above the water in the game. He stays anchored in the surf and slides along the
shore; the fight is about his reach.

What he does in the fight (design for this):
- **Tidal slam**: a long rubber-hose arm arcs over the beach and a giant **mitten hand** slams down (the arms are drawn
  by the game as a bendy chain; design the **hands** as separate big mittens).
- **Wave rings**: he slaps the sea and a crest rolls up the beach.
- **Ink rain**: cheeks puffed, he spits ink blobs.
- He summons kurage and kappa from the surf. Phase 2: the tide rises.

Identity to keep (every option):
- A huge, round, **dark-teal** sea spirit (a smooth bald dome of a head merging into a round body), seen from the chest
  up out of the water, with a foamy waterline.
- Big glowing **eyes** (the game makes them glow; design them clean white with dark pupils), a gentle sleepy smile,
  blush.
- A string of big **pearl prayer beads** (juzu) round his chest with one big pearl (gold) at the front.
- **Kelp** on his head.
- The mouth has three looks: the gentle **smile**, an **open** mouth (spitting), puffed **cheeks**.
- Personality: a drowsy, gentle giant who's grumpy to be woken.

Moving parts (separate pieces): the body (head + chest + beads + kelp, one piece); the **eyes** and **pupils** (the pupils
follow you); the mouth pieces (**smile**, **open mouth**, puffed **cheeks**: swapped by the game); the two **mitten hands**
(big separate hands: they slam). The water sheet and foam ring are the game's effects (don't design them in).

Signature poses (callouts): the **tidal slam** (a mitten raised high over the beach); the **ink spit** (cheeks puffed);
**sleepy** (eyes half-closed, a bubble).

## The three options (the same style, genuinely different designs)
- **Option A, "Sleepy Sea Monk"**: the current idea perfected: a smooth round dark-teal giant with big gentle eyes, the pearl juzu and kelp on his head.
- **Option B, "Barnacle Elder"**: an old sea monk: barnacles and little shells on his shoulders, a coral-bead juzu, droopy brows, a kelp beard.
- **Option C, "Sea Daruma"**: the most chibi: a daruma-shaped round sea spirit, a big round face, a tiny kelp topknot; the cutest.
