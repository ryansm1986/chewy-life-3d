# Concept brief: tsurara-concept (Pawhaven enemy series, Yukimi Onsen)

The series so far: the owner approved `approved-series-example-takenoko.png` (Bamboo Grove) and
`approved-series-example-kuri.png` (Momiji Hollow). Match their style, finish and sheet layout.

## The monster
**Tsurara** (icicle), an icicle wraith, the caster of Yukimi Onsen. Packs of 3–6. It **floats** about 0.4 m off the
ground; about **1.05 m** from the tail tip to the crown, the round head about 0.55 m across.

What it does in a fight (design for this):
- It floats at a distance and **blinks** around the fight (it shatters into glints and reappears elsewhere).
- It **raises its stubby arms** to call **icicle rain** down in circles round you.

Identity to keep (every option):
- A pale **ice ghost**: a round head and a body tapering to a **curling wisp tail** (#e6f6ff, shade #a6d8f6), slightly
  glassy, frosty.
- Big **glowing cyan eyes** (#7ae6ff) with catchlights, a small "o" mouth, a soft blush.
- **Icicle hair** hanging round the back and sides of its head (chunky rounded icicles, #9fe6ff to white tips) and a
  little **crystal crown** on top.
- Two stubby **icicle arms**.
- Personality: a shy, spooky-cute little frost spirit, a bit aloof.
- The game makes the eyes and the cyan crystals glow; design them as clear flat cyan shapes.

Moving parts (draw them as separate pieces):
- the **body** (head, face, body: one rigid piece);
- the **crown + icicle hair** (one piece on top of the head: it jitters when it casts);
- the **arms** (both together, one piece at the shoulders: they rise overhead to cast);
- the **tail** (the wisp, on a joint at the bottom of the body: it sways).

Signature attack pose callout: the **icicle call**: arms raised overhead, eyes blazing, icicles forming in the air above.

Variant strip (same model, colourways):
1. base: body #e6f6ff / #a6d8f6, crystals #9fe6ff, eyes #7ae6ff;
2. **Aurora Tsurara**: body #e8fff4 / #9ee0d0, crystals violet #c8a8ff, eyes mint #7affd8, blush lilac #d8b8ff;
3. **Black-ice Tsurara**: body #5a6ca8 / #2a3464, crystals #7ad8ff, eyes #6ef6ff, rim #0e1430, blush #7a9ae8.

## The three options (the same style, genuinely different designs)
- **Option A, "Icicle Wisp"**: the current idea perfected: a round ghostly head with a fringe of hanging icicle hair, a
  little crystal crown, a body narrowing to a curly wisp tail.
- **Option B, "Frost Hime"**: a little ice princess spirit: the icicle hair styled like a long hime-cut veil down the back,
  a taller crystal tiara, sleeve-like arms, a more elegant tail.
- **Option C, "Snowflake Bub"**: the most chibi: a round head that's nearly all face, a big snowflake-shaped crystal crown,
  tiny icicle tufts, tiny arms and a short curl of tail; the cutest.
