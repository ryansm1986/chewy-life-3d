# Concept brief: kamaitachi-concept (Pawhaven enemy series, Whispering Bamboo Grove)

## The monster
**Kamaitachi**, the sickle weasel of Japanese folklore, the fast melee enemy of the Whispering Bamboo Grove (the first
wild region). It's a **four-legged** weasel: about **0.75 m tall** at the head when standing on all fours, about
**1.0 m long** without the tail, plus a **big fluffy S-curved tail** as long again (about 0.9 m), which is its
silhouette from above.

What it does in a fight (design for this):
- It darts around you, low and quick.
- Its wind-up: it **rears up on its hind legs, sickles raised high**.
- Then it **dashes clean through you** in a straight line, leaving a cutting wind trail.

Identity to keep (every option):
- A long, low weasel or stoat: warm orange fur (#e0a45c) with a cream belly and muzzle (#fff4e2), a darker tail tip
  (#8a5a34), small round ears.
- **Crescent sickle blades on the forepaws** (#d6e2f0, toy-like: chunky, rounded tips, like a toy's plastic blade, a
  wooden or wrapped grip on the paw).
- A **wind scarf** (#5ab87a) round the neck, fluttering back.
- Personality: a cocky, scowling little speedster (current look: angry brows, blush).

Moving parts (separate pieces): the **body** (head, legs, sickles, scarf: one rigid piece) and the **tail** (pivot
at the tail root; it swishes and whips during the dash). The rear-up is done by tilting the whole body, so the design
must still look good **rotated 60° nose-up** (hind feet planted).

Signature attack pose callout: the **rear-up**: on its hind legs, both sickles high, the tail coiled behind, a wind
swirl at its feet.

Variant strip (same model, colourways):
1. base: fur #e0a45c, belly #fff4e2, tail tip #8a5a34, scarf #5ab87a, blades #d6e2f0;
2. **Yuki Kamaitachi** (snow weasel): fur #f2f0ec, belly #ffffff, tail tip #2e2834 (like a stoat's black tail tip),
   scarf #6aa8e8, blades #d0e8ff;
3. **Storm Kamaitachi** (zap): fur #8c7aa6, belly #ece4f6, tail tip #3a2a4a, scarf #e8503a, blades #ffd870, plus a
   **third blade on the tail tip** with a little lightning-bolt notch.

## The three options (the same style, genuinely different designs)
- **Option A, "Wind Ronin"**: the current idea perfected: a sleek stoat, a long flowing two-tailed scarf, sickles
  strapped to the forearms with cloth wraps, a big S-tail with a fluffy fan tip. Stylish and fast.
- **Option B, "Tumble Pup"**: rounder and more chibi: a big head (about 45 % of the body length), a short bean body,
  stubby legs, sickles like big curved leaves held in the paws, the tail shaped like a **whirlwind swirl** (a spiral
  of fur). The cutest, most toy-like.
- **Option C, "Bandit Weasel"**: a little bandit: a **knotted bandana mask** across the eyes (eyes still clear and big
  through it, or the mask above the eyes as a headband), a scarf, one big sickle and one small one, a scruffy
  three-tuft tail. Cheeky and roguish.
