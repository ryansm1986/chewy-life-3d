# Concept brief: kodama-concept (Pawhaven enemy series, Whispering Bamboo Grove)

## The monster
**Kodama**, a little tree spirit, the ranged fodder enemy of the Whispering Bamboo Grove (the first wild region).
About **1.0 m tall**, and it **floats** about 0.25 m off the ground (its little legs dangle).

What it does in a fight (design for this):
- It hovers, keeps its distance and **flicks volleys of leaf darts** at you.
- Its signature tell is **rattling its head** ("karakara"): the head wobbles and spins side to side on the body
  before a volley, so the **head is a separate piece** on a neck point.

Identity to keep (every option):
- A **big lumpy head** that's most of its mass (about 55–65 % of the height), pale white-green (#f3f6ec), on a small
  **bean-shaped body** with stubby arms and **dangling legs**.
- A **leaf sprout** on top of the head (#7ab04a): the signature from above.
- A sweet, slightly eerie-but-cute forest-spirit feel: wide eyes, a small "o" mouth. **Use the toy line's glossy
  eyes** (see the hero lineup), not hollow holes. **It must not look like Studio Ghibli's kodama** (no hollow-dot
  faces, no rattling skull-bodies copied from the film): an original design.

Moving parts (separate pieces): the **head** (pivot at the neck) and the **body** (with its arms and legs, one rigid
piece). Optionally a separate sprout on the head (it flutters).

Signature attack pose callout: the **rattle and throw**: head tipped and blurred with motion lines, one arm flicking
a fan of 3 glowing leaf darts.

Variant strip (same model, colourways):
1. base: skin #f3f6ec, sprout #7ab04a, darts #a8d85a;
2. **Moss Kodama**: skin #e6f0da with a cap of moss (#7ab04a) over the top of the head and a tiny red mushroom
   (#e8503a) growing from it;
3. **Elder Kodama**: skin #f6eedc, a little crown of pink sakura blossoms (#ffb8d8) instead of the sprout, and glowing
   soft-gold eyes (#ffd870).

## The three options (the same style, genuinely different designs)
- **Option A, "Rice-Cake Spirit"**: the current idea perfected: a smooth, softly lumpy round head like a mochi, a
  two-leaf sprout, a tiny pear body, short dangly legs, arms like little nubs. Simple and pure.
- **Option B, "Birch Bud"**: the head is a smooth rounded **wood knot** with a few soft bark-ring dents and a twig
  crown with 3–4 leaves, a little **leaf cape or collar** round the neck, root-tendril legs that taper and curl. More
  "tree" and woodsy.
- **Option C, "Seed Lantern"**: the head shaped like an **acorn or seed pod** (a cap with a stalk on top, the face on
  the smooth nut), a faint inner glow in the cheeks, a body like a little dangling root bundle, leaf-shaped hands it
  throws darts with. A brighter, more magical-lantern read.
