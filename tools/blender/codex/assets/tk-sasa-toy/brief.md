# Asset brief: tk-sasa-toy (VL-2: the Blender Grandma Sasa, phase 1: the model)

Read `../villager-toy-common.md` first: the shared technical rules, the lessons and the process rules. **Two differences
from it**:
- This is a **zone villager** (Takemori Village, the bamboo zone), not one of Blossom Hollow's seven.
- The sheet `refs/sheet.png` was drawn for this task (2026-11-02) and approved by the director. The owner delegated the
  pick. It is the blueprint: don't redesign, just build it beautifully.
`refs/current-*-portrait.png` (where present) is the procedural kit version in the game now: identity only.

## Who
**Grandma Sasa**, Takemori Village's elder, a soft, round grandmother giant panda who keeps the bamboo grove. Kind, wise, a little mischievous.
The blueprint is `refs/sheet.png` ("Grandma Sasa - Grove Elder").

## Must keep (from the sheet)
- **Fur** cream-white #FBF8F2; the panda's **patches** #2C2830: round, slightly drooping eye patches, the ears, the arms and the legs and feet; a small round cream tail (back view).
- **Face**: warm amber-brown eyes #8A6A3A inside the dark patches (the eye whites and lash line must still read against the dark patch, so the sclera is generous); soft brows; a small dark nose and a gentle smile on a short cream muzzle; blush #F0A0A4.
- **Kimono top** #7AA874 with wide short sleeves; a **mustard obi** #E8C870 as a thick soft band; a **pleated moss skirt** #45563F to the ankles with broad rounded pleats.
- **Cream kerchief** #EFE4CF round the neck with a round knot and two short rounded ends at the front.
- Her **bamboo walking stick** (#6F8950, a separate object in her right paw is optional; leave it off the rig, the game can add it later).

## Sizes
- Total height **1.00 m** (the sheet's note). Villagers stand about 1.0–1.15 m to the crown.
- Measure everything else on the sheet in Step 0 (`measure.md`, fractions of the head width W). The director checks your
  table against the sheet.

## Done when
As in the shared brief:
- within about 5% of your measured targets;
- appealing in `portrait.png`;
- the outfit and accessories reading well in `game.png` from both 45° yaws;
- ≤ 30k triangles, one atlas, `joints.json`.
