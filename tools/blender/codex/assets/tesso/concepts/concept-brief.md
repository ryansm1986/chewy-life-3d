# Concept brief: tesso-concept (Pawhaven enemy series, Maple Roots)

The series so far: the owner approved `approved-series-example-takenoko.png` (Bamboo Grove) and
`approved-series-example-kuri.png` (Momiji Hollow). Match their style, finish and sheet layout.

## The monster
**Tesso**, the iron-rat yōkai (a monk turned into a rat with teeth of iron), the burrowing ambusher **tank** of Maple
Roots (the zone dungeon under Momiji Hollow). Packs of 1–3. Sits on its haunches about **1.0 m tall**.

What it does in a fight (design for this):
- **Burrow** (mid range): it scrabbles into the earth and a **mound of dirt** races along under the floor after you; the
  mound swells and shakes, and it **bursts** up out of it, then sits dazed, shaking the dirt off.
- **Gnaw** (close): it rears back, **iron buck teeth** bared, then lunges in a chattering flurry of bites.

Identity to keep (every option):
- A round, chubby **grey rat** sat on its haunches (fur #7c808e, light #a8aebc, belly #dcd4cc), **huge round ears** (pink
  insides #f2a0aa), a pink nose and whiskers, a long curling pink **tail** (#d8a8a8).
- Two big shiny **iron buck teeth** (steel grey with a highlight).
- A monk's **juzu** of wooden beads round its neck (#6a4030) with a red tassel (#d8402e).
- Little front **paws** held up in front.
- Personality: a greedy, twitchy, nibbly little rascal.

Moving parts (draw them as separate pieces):
- the **body** (torso, haunches, feet, tail, juzu: one rigid piece);
- the **head** (ears, face, teeth), on a neck joint: it rears back and lunges;
- the **paws** (both front paws, one piece at the chest);
- the **dirt mound** (a separate low mound of earth with a few leaves, as a callout: what you see while it tunnels).

Signature attack pose callout: the **burst**: erupting up out of the dirt mound, clods and leaves flying.

Variant strip (same model, colourways):
1. base: as above;
2. **Sutra Tesso**: brown fur #8a6e5c / #b89c86, belly #ecdcc8, dark beads #3e2a20 and an orange tassel #e8a03a, a gnawed
   sutra **scroll** in its paws;
3. **Elder Tesso** (the monk Raigō himself): white fur #cfc8c4 / #f4f0ec, a saffron **kesa** sash across the chest, bushy
   white brows, a bigger juzu with a red tassel.

## The three options (the same style, genuinely different designs)
- **Option A, "Iron-Tooth Rat"**: the current idea perfected: a pear-shaped chubby rat, giant round ears, the iron teeth,
  the juzu.
- **Option B, "Rat Monk"**: more monk: a little straw hat and kesa, the juzu worn like a bandolier, a serious face (until
  the iron teeth come out).
- **Option C, "Dumpling Rat"**: the most chibi: a near-round ball body, enormous ears, a tiny face with the two iron teeth,
  tiny paws; the cutest.
