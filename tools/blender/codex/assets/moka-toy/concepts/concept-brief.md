# Concept brief: moka-concept (Toybox Chibi)

## The character
**Moka** is the game's second playable hero: a **Boykin Spaniel mage** who arrived in Blossom Hollow after reading about the
Burrow. She casts dog-themed **water, starlight and duck-hunt magic** (Splash Bolt, Great Wave, a spectral retriever) with a
staff. Bright, bookish, a little bossy with Chewy, she adores water and ducks.

She's a **biped like Chewy**, about **1.05 m to the top of her head** in the game world, with the wizard hat adding about
0.25–0.3 m on top. The player switches between her and Chewy, so the two stand side by side all the time.

The game now has a **Toybox Chibi** art style:
- the new Chewy: `sheet-B.png` (his approved model sheet) and `chewy-toy-game-crop.png` (him in the game);
- the new Rosie: `rosie-sheet-A.png`;
- the new Shadow: `shadow-sheet-A.png`.

**Moka must look like the same toy line**, as if all four were sold together. Deliver **3 design options**. The owner picks
one, and it's then modelled in Blender.

`moka-current-turnaround.png`, `moka-current-headshots.png` and `moka-current-game.png` are her current model. Keep the
**identity** (the costume idea, the colours, the spaniel ears), **not the look**: its narrow, realistic long-snouted dog face
and stick-thin limbs are exactly what the new design replaces.

## Style (fixed for every option: match sheet-B and the other two sheets)
- A vinyl-toy / Pokopia / Animal Crossing feel.
  - **Proportions**: about 2.4 heads tall (hat not counted); a big rounded head (a squircle, slightly wider than tall); a
    stubby, rounded body and limbs; mitten paws; broad, rounded feet.
  - **Surfaces**: soft, matte and smooth, with gentle painted shading. Fur is **a few big sculpted, smooth masses**, never strands.
- The **face language of sheet-B**, on a spaniel:
  - big glossy eyes set low and wide, facing a little outward: a warm-white sclera showing white crescents, a **golden-amber
    iris** (#e8a93a) tucked toward the nose, a large dark pupil, two catchlights, and a thick upper lash line with a small wing
    and **2–3 little lashes** (she's a girl);
  - soft arched brows;
  - a **short, rounded toy muzzle** (like Chewy's, not a long realistic snout) with a rounded **liver nose** (#4a2418) and a
    highlight;
  - a gentle closed smile;
  - blush dabs.
- Clean colour blocking with crisp borders; a simple palette with hex swatches on the sheet.
- It must read from the game camera (45° down, about 20 m away): the **hat, the long curly ears and the seafoam-and-lavender
  costume** are the signature. **The hat brim must never hide the eyes from 45° above**: keep the brim small, or tipped back.

## Identity (every option must keep these)
- **Boykin Spaniel**: a solid rich **chocolate/liver** coat (#7a4a34, with curl highlights #8c5a40 and shadow #603a26),
  **no white markings**.
- **Long pendant ears covered in big wavy curls**, hanging to about chin or shoulder level. They're her strongest silhouette
  feature: thick, soft, clay-like curl masses, never thin flaps.
- A little **curly topknot or fringe** of the same curls peeking out under the hat.
- **Golden-amber eyes**, a **liver nose**, and a **stubby, happy tail** with a curl tuft.
- **Not Chewy.** Chewy is also a chocolate dog. He has short folded ears, a white chest blaze and white chin, a navy gi and a
  red scarf. Moka must read differently at a glance: long curly ears, an all-liver coat, a mage costume in cool seafoam and
  lavender, and the hat.
- **Mage costume colours**:
  - seafoam / teal (#3fb0a0 hat, #4fc4b4 capelet);
  - lavender robe (#b8a4e8);
  - a violet sash (#8a6ad8);
  - gold trim and stars (#f4c04a);
  - a tiny **rubber-duck** charm (#ffd24a with an orange bill).
- **A wizard hat** in every option: floppy, with a bent tip.
- **Her staff** (a driftwood crook cradling a glowing seafoam orb, a blue ribbon and a duck charm) is a **separate game prop**,
  so it must not be part of the body:
  - show it only as a small callout;
  - draw the turnaround **without** it, the right paw a soft closed mitten that could grip it.
- Personality: bright, bookish, a little bossy and proud, a water-and-ducks enthusiast. Show it in the expressions.

## The three options (the same style, genuinely different designs)
- **Option A, "Star Apprentice"**: the faithful update of her current look.
  - A seafoam **hooded capelet** (hood down) with gold trim and a gold **star clasp**, over a lavender knee-length robe with
    little gold stars.
  - A violet sash with the rubber-duck charm.
  - A floppy seafoam wizard hat with a violet band, a **paw-print patch** and a bent tip.
- **Option B, "Puddle Mage"**: the water-and-ducks side of her.
  - A seafoam **rain-cape / slicker** with a big round hood (down) and toggle clasps.
  - A shorter lavender tunic-dress with a **bubble or water-drop** pattern instead of stars.
  - **Duck-yellow (#ffd24a) rain boots**.
  - A rounder, softer "rain-witch" hat with a gently drooping brim and a **tiny rubber duck riding on it**.
  - A shell- or drop-shaped clasp.
- **Option C, "Star Scholar"**: the bookish side, with the bolder silhouette.
  - A layered lavender **scholar robe** (to mid-calf, with the feet still clearly visible) with a seafoam **stole** and gold
    tassels.
  - A small **book satchel** on the hip with a star on its flap.
  - **Round reading glasses tucked into the hat band**, not on her face, so the eyes stay clear.
  - A taller pointed hat with a **crescent-moon charm** hanging from its bent tip.
  - The curly **ears tied near the tips with seafoam ribbons**, so they swing like two pigtails.

## Practical constraints (she's animated in the game)
- She walks, runs and swings a staff, and raises both arms to cast. Her costume must allow that:
  - the robe or dress is an A-line that clears the legs at the knee;
  - the capelet or cape doesn't cover the upper arms' swing;
  - nothing long trails on the ground.
- **The arms and legs must stay visible**: chunky toy limbs, like Chewy's.
- The hat sits **firmly on the head**: one rigid shape with the ears coming out underneath it.
- The ears hang and swing, so show them clearly in the side and back views.

## Sheet requirements
One landscape sheet per option, laid out **like sheet-B**:
- an **A-pose turnaround** in **front, 3/4, side and back** views, consistent with each other, on a common ground line;
- a **head close-up**, front and 3/4, with **3 expressions**:
  - **happy** (eyes closed in a smile);
  - **casting** (determined, mouth open as if shouting "Splash!");
  - **smug / bossy** (one brow up, a proud little smile);
- a **casting pose** callout: both arms raised, holding the staff;
- the **staff** as a small callout;
- hex swatches;
- a light, flat, evenly lit background.

## Done when
- 3 sheets, `concepts/option-A.png`, `option-B.png` and `option-C.png`, plus `concepts/notes.md`.
- Each one reads unmistakably as **Moka**: a chocolate Boykin Spaniel mage with long curly ears, amber eyes and the seafoam
  hat. Each also reads as the same toy line as Chewy, Rosie and Shadow, and the three are clearly different designs.
- The four views agree well enough to model from. Her ears, the hat and the legs under the robe must be clear in the side and
  back views.
