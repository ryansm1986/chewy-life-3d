## Shared identity and modelling targets

Moka is an all-liver Boykin Spaniel girl with a short rounded toy muzzle, amber eyes, broad mitten paws, chunky limbs, long sculpted pendant curls, a small curly fringe and a stubby curled tail. No white fur markings. Warm-white areas are eye sclera only.

All options target approximately **2.4 heads tall without the hat**: head-to-remaining-body ratio **1:1.4**, or head height about **0.4375 m** within **1.05 m** ground-to-head height. The numbers are modelling targets; illustrations are visual blueprints, not dimensioned orthographic CAD. Keep the head slightly wider than tall. Make fur from a handful of smooth sculpted masses, not individual strands.

Lift the hat's front brim above the forehead and tip the hat back. The eyes are exposed in the supplied views; the exact 45-degree gameplay-camera clearance still needs to be verified on the eventual 3D model. Use the printed hex values as material targets rather than sampling painted lighting from the figures.

Shared face/prop accents: sclera #FFF6E8; pupil/lid #211811; blush #D98D7E; duck bill #E99536; driftwood staff #C7A676; orb #A7F0DF; staff ribbon #548ED1. Eyes should have two catchlights and a thick upper lash line with 2–3 short outer lashes.

The staff is a separate prop: a driftwood crook around a round luminous seafoam orb, a blue ribbon and a duck charm. The neutral turnaround contains no staff; the casting callout shows its grip. Do not incorporate the shaft into the body.
