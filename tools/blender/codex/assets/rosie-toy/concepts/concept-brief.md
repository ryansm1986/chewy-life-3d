# Concept brief: rosie-concept (Toybox Chibi)

## The character
**Rosie** is Chewy's best human friend and the village's little baker. She runs "Rosie's Treats" in Blossom Hollow
(strawberry mochi, daifuku), welcomes Chewy home, sends him on quests and patches him up when Shadow drags him home
after a lost fight. She's a **young girl**: sweet, cheerful, a bit bossy and very warm, with a kid's voice. In the game
world she stands about **1.25 m tall** to the crown, just a little taller than the Toybox Chewy.

The game now has a **Toybox Chibi** art style. Its first character is the new Chewy: the attached `sheet-B.png` (his
approved model sheet) and `chewy-toy-game-crop.png` (him in the game). **Rosie must look like the same toy line**, as if
Chewy and Rosie were sold side by side. Deliver **3 design options**. The owner picks one, and it's then modelled in
Blender. `rosie-current-crop.png` is her current in-game model: keep her identity, but not its look. It has adult-ish
proportions, small sleepy eyes and thin limbs, all of which the new design replaces. `rosie-older-version.png` is an
older toy version from an earlier game, a useful hint at how simple she can be.

## Style (fixed for every option: match sheet-B)
- A vinyl-toy / Pokopia / Animal Crossing feel.
  - **Proportions**: about **2.4 heads tall**; a big rounded head (a squircle, slightly wider than tall); a stubby,
    rounded body and limbs; mitten-like hands with a small thumb; small rounded shoes.
  - **Surfaces**: soft, matte, smooth surfaces with gentle painted shading.
  - **Hair**: a few **big sculpted, smooth masses** (curls read as big round lobes, not strands).
- The **face language of sheet-B**, adapted to a human girl:
  - **big glossy eyes set low and wide**: a warm-white sclera showing white crescents, the iris tucked toward the nose,
    a large dark pupil, two catchlights, and a thick dark upper lash line with a small wing, **plus 2–3 cute lash
    flicks** at the outer corner;
  - soft arched brows;
  - a tiny rounded button nose;
  - a gentle closed smile;
  - round pink blush dabs (#ff9ab0).
- Clean colour blocking with crisp borders; a simple palette with hex swatches on the sheet.
- It must read from the game camera (45° down, about 20 m away): a strong silhouette (her curly hair and the bow are
  the signature) and no thin details.

## Identity (every option must keep these)
- A human girl, peachy skin **#ffe4d2**; warm brown eyes **#7a4424** (iris), round blush.
- **Curly chestnut-brown hair**: #6b3a22 with #8e5634 highlight lobes. It's voluminous and curly, her signature shape.
- A **red bow** (#e8364a) in her hair.
- A **pink dress** (#ff8fb0) with **white** trim or collar (#fff6f0).
- **White socks and red shoes** (#d8443a).
- She's a baker, so a hint of it in every option (a strawberry motif, flour on her cheek, an apron or a treat).
- Personality: bright, kind, a little bossy. Show it in the expressions (happy, pouty-bossy, surprised).

## The three options (the same style, genuinely different designs)
- **Option A, "Sweetheart"**: the faithful update.
  - Hair: a big curly bob with the red bow on top.
  - The pink dress with puffy short sleeves, a white rounded collar and a white scalloped hem.
  - White socks and red Mary-Jane shoes.
  - A small strawberry brooch.
  - Closest to her current identity.
- **Option B, "Village Baker"**: her role in the costume.
  - Hair: her curly hair in a puffy low ponytail with the red bow, and a small white baker's bandana.
  - A pink dress under a **white apron** with a strawberry pocket and a little flour dust.
  - She holds a small tray of mochi (a prop callout).
- **Option C, "Strawberry Ribbon"**: the bolder silhouette.
  - Hair: **two big round curly buns** (like pom-poms) on top of her head, with a big red ribbon between them.
  - A pink pinafore over a white puff-sleeved blouse, with strawberry buttons.
  - A little satchel of treats.
  - The most distinctive from the game camera.

## Sheet requirements
One landscape sheet per option, laid out **like sheet-B**:
- an A-pose turnaround in front, 3/4, side and back views, all consistent with each other;
- a head close-up, front and 3/4, with 2–3 expressions;
- hex swatches;
- her signature prop as a small callout.
Use a light, flat, evenly lit background.

## Done when
- 3 sheets, `concepts/option-A.png`, `option-B.png` and `option-C.png`, plus `concepts/notes.md`.
- Each one reads unmistakably as **Rosie** and as the same toy line as sheet-B (same eye construction, proportions and
  finish), and the three are clearly different designs.
- The views agree well enough to model from.
