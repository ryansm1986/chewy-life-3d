# Concept brief: golden-concept (Toybox Chibi)

## The character
The **Golden Dragoon** (working name; the owner names him later, so label the sheets "GOLDEN DRAGOON · Option A/B/C") is
a new playable hero, the game's **dragoon** class. He's a **male Golden Retriever**.
- **He fights with a long spear made of dog toys** and throws **javelins**, with high leaping dive attacks, dragoon
  style.
- **His dragon pet** is **Shadow**, the cast's Boston terrier, in a **little dragon whelp outfit**, flying beside him.
- **His personality**: sunny, brave and boundlessly enthusiastic, a "big golden puppy" who believes he's a legendary
  dragon knight, with a heart of gold.
- The game is cozy and cute: dragon knight energy, toy-box charm.

He's a **biped like Chewy**, about **1.0–1.05 m to the top of the head**, plus his helmet crest. He stands beside the other
heroes, so match the attached cast:
- `sheet-B.png`, `chewy-toy-game-crop.png` and `chewy-samurai-sheet-E.png`: Chewy, now a black-and-gold samurai;
- `moka-sheet.png`: Moka, a mage;
- `poe-sheet-D.png`: Poe, a black pug ninja;
- `shadow-sheet-A.png`: **Shadow, a Boston terrier**;
- `cast-lineup.png`: the cast in the game.

**He must look like the same toy line.** Deliver **3 design options**. The owner picks one, and it's modelled in Blender.

## Style (fixed for every option: match sheet-B and the other sheets)
- A vinyl-toy / Pokopia / Animal Crossing feel: about 2.4 heads tall; a big rounded head; stubby rounded body and limbs;
  mitten paws; broad rounded feet. Soft, matte, smooth surfaces with gentle painted shading.
- **Golden Retriever fur as a few big sculpted, smooth masses, never strands**:
  - long, soft, **feathered floppy ears**;
  - a fluffy ruff at the chest;
  - feathering on the backs of the legs;
  - a big plumed, waggy tail.
  - A friendly medium muzzle, toy-short like Chewy's.
- **The face language of sheet-B**: big, glossy, warm brown eyes with white crescents and catchlights, and a thick upper
  lash line.
  - **The eyes sit mid-face, above the muzzle.**
  - A rounded dark nose, a big happy open smile as his default, blush dabs.
- Clean colour blocking, 6–8 colours with hex swatches. It must read from the game camera (35° down, about 20 m).
  **The helmet must never hide the eyes from above**: keep it an open face, a circlet or a crest.

## Identity (every option)
- **A male Golden Retriever dragoon**: dragon-knight armour that's light, smooth and rounded, with **dragon motifs**
  (scale-pattern plates, little horn or wing flares on the helmet, a dragon-crest tabard) in the option's dragon colours.
- **Signature weapon: the Toy Lance.** It's a **separate game prop**, so show it only as a callout, and draw the turnaround
  without it in the paws.
  - A long spear, about 1.6× his height.
  - A shaft of **braided rope toy**, wrapped in bands.
  - A **squeaky rubber spearhead** shaped like a stylised dragon fang or flame.
  - A **tennis-ball pommel**.
  - A small **dragon-wing guard** of plush felt.
- **Javelins**: **squeaky toy darts** (rubber-tipped, with felt fletching) in a back quiver. Show the quiver on the
  turnaround and one javelin as a callout.
- **Shadow the dragon whelp** callout: one per sheet, the same Shadow as `shadow-sheet-A.png` (his face, black-and-white
  coat and bat ears unchanged) **wearing a cute dragon whelp costume** in the option's colours:
  - a soft hood with two little horns and his ears through slits;
  - felt **wings** that flap;
  - a little spiked tail cover with soft felt spikes;
  - a belly-scale bib.
  Show him **flying**, wings out, front paws tucked, and from the front and side. He's still a dog in a costume: cute, not a
  real dragon.
- **Not Chewy** (black and gold), **not Poe** (black and moss green), **not Moka** (seafoam and lavender).
- **Personality** in the expressions: heroic, beaming, and an excited puppy.

## The three options (the same style, genuinely different designs, a different coat each)
- **Option A, "Crimson Wyrm Knight"**: a **classic gold** coat (#d9a050, light #f0cf8a).
  - Crimson (#c43a3a) and cream (#f4e8d0) dragon armour with **bronze** (#b07a3a) trim.
  - A red dragon-crest tabard.
  - A helmet circlet with two small swept-back horns.
  - Shadow's whelp suit is crimson with cream wings.
- **Option B, "Skyfang Wyvern Rider"**: a **light cream** coat (#f2dcae, light #fbeed2).
  - Sky blue (#5aa8e0) and white armour with **silver** (#c8d0dc) trim, and wyvern-wing pauldrons.
  - A short flowing scarf like a wind ribbon.
  - Shadow's whelp suit is sky blue with white wings.
- **Option C, "Emberleaf Dragon Guard"**: a **deep red-gold** coat (#c47a3a, light #e0a868).
  - The bold silhouette: emerald (#3a9a6a) scale armour with **warm brass** (#c8a050) trim.
  - A tall dragon-head helm crest, with the face fully open.
  - A layered tabard with a leaf-dragon emblem.
  - Shadow's whelp suit is emerald with gold felt spikes.

## Practical constraints (he's animated)
- He walks, runs, thrusts and sweeps a long spear, leaps high and dives, and throws javelins one-armed. The armour must
  allow it.
- **The arms and legs stay visible.** The tabard stops above the knees.
- Ears, ruff and tail are chunky volumes. Nothing thin or long.
- **Shadow's whelp wings and tail** are chunky felt shapes that read from the game camera and can flap.

## Sheet requirements
One landscape sheet per option, laid out like sheet-B:
- a **front, 3/4, side and back A-pose turnaround** on a common ground line;
- **head close-ups** (front and 3/4) with 3 expressions: **heroic** (a determined grin), **happy** (eyes closed, tongue
  out) and **puppy-excited**;
- two **action poses**: a **leaping dive** with the lance pointed down, and a **javelin throw**;
- **callouts**: the Toy Lance, a javelin, and **Shadow the dragon whelp flying** (front and side);
- hex swatches;
- a light, flat background.

## Done when
- 3 sheets, `concepts/option-A.png`, `option-B.png` and `option-C.png`, plus `concepts/notes.md`.
- Each reads unmistakably as a **cute male Golden Retriever dragoon**: the feathered ears, the ruff, the plumed tail, the
  dragon armour, the toy lance and javelins. Each sheet shows **Shadow as a recognisable Boston terrier in a whelp
  costume**. Each reads as the same toy line as the cast.
- The four views agree well enough to model from.
