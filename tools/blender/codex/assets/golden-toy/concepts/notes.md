## Shared construction and reference priorities

- Use smooth rounded volumes, not hair strands. Ear feathering, calf tufts, chest ruff and tail are a few broad sculpted masses; simplify the small painted ridges shown in A and C.
- Mitten hands and broad paw feet stay visible. Tabards end above the knees. Shoulder caps, hip panels and forearm caps should leave joints clear for throws, leaps and spear sweeps.
- The Toy Lance is a separate prop. Its shaft is a thick braided rope toy with broad bands; the tip is squeaky rubber, guard plush felt, and pommel a tennis ball.
- Shadow's white forehead blaze/muzzle, slate coat, upright pink-lined bat ears, blue collar and gold tag are identity anchors. Hood horns are separate from his ears. Wings and tail spikes are thick felt costume pieces.
- Shadow's identity colours follow the supplied sheet-A: slate #34303F, white #F4ECE0, brown/amber eyes #C88732, nose #212D28, inner ears #F0A0A8, blue collar #4AA8F0 and gold tag #FFD24A. These belong to the companion and are not additional hero costume colours.
- Swatch hex codes above define intended base colours; the images include gentle shading, dark pupils and white eye catchlights.
- The prop, portrait, expression and companion callouts are independently scaled for legibility. Use the written metre dimensions, not their page size, to model the lance.
- These are concept blueprints rather than dimensionally exact orthographic drawings. Minor perspective and sculptural differences remain, most visibly the shorter apparent front crest in C. Build one 0.16 m crest using C's side/three-quarter shape and keep that height consistent in the 3D model. All requested view/panel types are present.
