# Round 2 feedback (golden-toy): about 7/10; the face and costume layout are good, the proportions need the cast's

## Approved and locked
- The build approach and determinism.
- The face construction: the big brown lens eyes facing about 30° out, the happy open-mouth smile with the lip roll, the
  cavity and tongue (lovely), the nose with its highlight.
- The costume layout: the scale chest plate, the belt and medallion, the leaf-dragon tabard and tassets, the bracers, the
  quiver.
- The lance and javelin props, and the toon band numbers.

## Your two decisions
1. **Head size: use the cast's proportions, not the sheet's 3.1 heads.** He must stand beside Chewy, Moka and Poe as the
   same toy line (about 2.4 heads). Next to Chewy his head reads tiny (`cmp-cast.png`).
   - Scale the head (W) up to about **0.60 W of Chewy's 0.675 m head**, i.e. about **0.40 m → about 0.60 m**. Keep the
     body.
   - Re-fit the helmet, ears, ruff and neck to the bigger head, and re-measure the face in W units: the fractions stay the
     same.
   - Keep the crown at about 1.20 m by shortening the body a little if needed. Compare against chewy-b and Poe in
     `cmp-cast.png`.
2. **The face rule: follow the sheet** (`EZW`, `NZW`, `NHH` back to the sheet). The "iris bottom at or above the nose top"
   rule came from Moka's long muzzle and doesn't apply to his medium toy muzzle. Match the sheet's nose height and size.

## Fixes
3. **Ears: fluffy feathered locks, not paddles.** They read as flat crescents with faceted rims.
   - Give them the sheet's **volume and layered wavy locks**: 5–6 lobes, with the outer edge broken into soft feathered
     tufts, thicker (about 1.5×) and rounder in section.
   - From the back they should read as two big fluffy masses, not broad discs.
   - Add rim resolution, spending triangles from the helmet or the body.
4. **Colour**:
   - The emerald reads **minty**. Push it to the sheet's deeper **#3a9a6a**, with a darker shade #2a7a54 in the scale
     shadows.
   - The brass reads **pale yellow**. Warm it to the sheet's **#c8a050**, with #a07a30 in shadows.
   - Check in the game light that the emerald stays clearly bluer and brighter than Poe's moss green.
5. **The helmet's dragon**: make it about **1.5× bigger** and closer to the sheet, sitting on the crown with its head
   forward and tail curling into the crest, so it reads at play distance. The crest fins can be a touch shorter and rounder.
6. **Feathering**: the arms and thighs read as smooth orange tubes. The sheet has **fluffy feathering**: soft fur tufts on
   the backs of the forearms, the elbows and the thighs, plus fluffy fur cuffs at the wrists and ankles above the light-fur
   paws. Add them as a few sculpted masses each.
7. **The ruff**: bigger and fluffier, as on the sheet. Smooth the lumpy surface.
8. **The tail**: a big fluffy plume like the sheet, raised, with layered feathered locks and a lighter tip. It currently
   reads as a flat orange shape.
9. **Smaller**: pauldrons about 10% narrower (to 0.582), the near eye's inner corner rounded at 3/4, and the feet lifted 8 mm
   to sit on z = 0.
