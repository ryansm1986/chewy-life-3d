# Concept brief: yukidaruma-concept (Pawhaven enemy series, Yukimi Onsen)

The series so far: the owner approved `approved-series-example-takenoko.png` (Bamboo Grove) and
`approved-series-example-kuri.png` (Momiji Hollow). Match their style, finish and sheet layout.

## The monster
**Yuki-daruma**, the Japanese two-ball snowman, the heavy brute of Yukimi Onsen. Packs of 2–5. About **1.65 m tall** with
its bucket; the bottom ball about 1.1 m across.

What it does in a fight (design for this):
- It lumbers after you; from range it **curls up into a giant rolling snowball** and **bowls through you** (the snowman
  vanishes into a big round snowball with its scarf band, a twig and a mitten poking out), then pops out **dizzy**.
- Up close it **raises both mitten fists** overhead and **frost-slams** the ground.

Identity to keep (every option):
- Two lumpy **snowballs** (snow #f6faff, cool shade #c2d4ee underneath), the head on top.
- **Coal eyes** with sparkles under thick angry charcoal **brows** (#2c2934), a **carrot nose** (orange), a coal-bead
  grin, coal buttons, blush.
- A tipped red **bucket** for a helmet (#d8453a, darker top rim), a striped green **scarf** (#3a9a5a with cream stripes).
- **Twig arms** (#7a5236) ending in big green knitted **mitten fists**.
- Personality: a grumpy, tough-guy snowman with a soft side.

Moving parts (draw them as separate pieces):
- the **body** (both snowballs, face, bucket, scarf: one rigid piece);
- the **two arms** (twig + mitten), each on its own shoulder joint on the bottom ball: they raise overhead for the slam;
- the **rolled-up snowball** (a separate big round snowball with the scarf wrapped round it, a twig and a mitten poking
  out, a bit of the bucket showing): shown instead of the snowman while it rolls. Draw it as a callout.

Signature attack pose callout: the **frost-slam**: both mittens raised overhead, then smashed down with a burst of snow.

Variant strip (same model, colourways):
1. base: as above;
2. **Dusk Daruma**: blue bucket #4a6ad8 / #3a54b0, red scarf #e8503a with cream stripes #fff2d8, red mittens;
3. **Grand Daruma**: gold bucket #e8b83a / #c8942a, purple scarf #8a4ab8 with gold stripes #ffe8a0, purple mittens, whiter
   snow #fbf8ff with lilac shade #cfc6ee.

## The three options (the same style, genuinely different designs)
- **Option A, "Bucket Brute"**: the current idea perfected: a big bottom ball, a smaller scowling head, the bucket tipped
  over one brow, long twig arms with huge mitten fists.
- **Option B, "Sumo Snowman"**: a squat, heavy, pear-shaped snowman (head sunk into the body), a short thick scarf tied
  like a sumo belt-knot, stubby twig arms with giant mittens, a fierce determined face.
- **Option C, "Snow Daifuku"**: the most chibi: two very round, soft balls, the head nearly as big as the body, a tiny
  bucket perched on top, a big pouty face; the cutest.
