# Concept brief: shihtzu-concept (Toybox Chibi)

## The character
The **Shih Tzu Knight** (working name; the owner names him later, so label the sheets "SHIH TZU KNIGHT · Option A/B/C") is
a new playable hero, the game's **dark knight** class. He's a **male Shih Tzu**.
- **He fights with a big flail made of dog toys.**
- **He casts dark dog magic**: shadowy curses that deal **damage over time**, ghostly bone spirits, gloom auras and other
  "dark arts".
- **The tone**: this is a cozy, cute game, so his darkness is **spooky-cute, not edgy**. Think a Halloween plush, or a
  gentle, slightly dramatic little knight who takes his "Order of the Midnight Paw" very seriously. Never scary, gory or
  menacing. He's loyal and soft-hearted under the gloom, and he loves naps and treats.

He's a **biped like Chewy**, about **1.0 m to the top of the head**, plus his topknot. He stands beside the other heroes,
so match the attached cast:
- `sheet-B.png`, `chewy-toy-game-crop.png` and `chewy-samurai-sheet-E.png`: Chewy, now a black-and-gold samurai;
- `moka-sheet.png`: Moka, a mage;
- `poe-sheet-D.png`: Poe, a black pug ninja;
- `shadow-sheet-A.png`: Shadow, a Boston terrier pet;
- `cast-lineup.png`: the cast in the game.

**He must look like the same toy line.** Deliver **3 design options**. The owner picks one, and it's modelled in Blender.

## Style (fixed for every option: match sheet-B and the other sheets)
- A vinyl-toy / Pokopia / Animal Crossing feel: about 2.4 heads tall; a big rounded head; stubby rounded body and limbs;
  mitten paws; broad rounded feet. Soft, matte, smooth surfaces with gentle painted shading.
- **Shih Tzu fur as a few big sculpted, smooth masses, never strands**:
  - a **topknot** tied up with a ribbon or cord (a signature Shih Tzu look);
  - the long drapey **ear fur** as soft locks;
  - a fluffy **"chrysanthemum" face**: puffs radiating round the face, a little moustache and beard under the nose;
  - a plumed tail curling over the back.
- **The face language of sheet-B**: big, round, glossy eyes with white crescents and catchlights, and a thick upper lash
  line.
  - **The eyes sit mid-face, above the short muzzle**: the iris bottom at or above the nose top, never on the cheeks.
  - A small rounded nose, a soft smile, blush dabs.
  - The face fluff must **never cover the eyes**.
- Clean colour blocking, 6–8 colours with hex swatches. It must read from the game camera (35° down, about 20 m).
  **No helmet or hood may hide the eyes from above.**

## Identity (every option)
- **A male Shih Tzu dark knight**: soft, rounded **toy armour** (smooth plates, no sharp spikes), a short cape or tabard,
  and the topknot always visible.
- **Signature weapon: the Toy Flail.** It's a **separate game prop**, so show it only as a callout, and draw the turnaround
  without it in the paws (soft closed mittens).
  - A chunky handle like a **chew bone**.
  - A **braided rope-toy chain**.
  - A **squeaky spiky-ball toy** head: soft rubber nubs, not blades, glowing faintly with dark magic.
  - Big and satisfying, about as long as he is tall when swung.
- **Dark dog magic** callout: show 2–3 spell looks in the option's glow colour:
  - a **curse mark**: a dark paw-print sigil that clings to a foe and drips little gloom wisps (damage over time);
  - **ghost bones** or **spirit pup wisps**;
  - a **gloom aura** ring.
  Cute-spooky, readable, and soft-edged.
- **Not Chewy** (black and gold samurai), **not Poe** (a black pug in moss green), **not Moka** (seafoam and lavender).
  - **Palette family**: midnight blue, deep plum or charcoal-violet armour with **silver** trim and a **violet, teal or
    moonlight glow** for the magic. Don't use gold as the main trim, since that's Chewy's.
- **Personality** in the expressions: solemn and dramatic ("the darkness compels me…"), but secretly sweet.

## The three options (the same style, genuinely different designs, a different coat each)
- **Option A, "Midnight Paladin"**: **gold-and-white** Shih Tzu (cream #f2e2c4, gold #d9a35a).
  - Rounded midnight-blue (#2a3058) plate armour with silver (#c8ccd8) trim.
  - A short **violet** (#7a4ac8) cape with a crescent-moon paw crest.
  - A small open-face helmet or circlet with little bat-wing flares. The topknot ribbon is violet.
  - The magic glows violet.
- **Option B, "Gloomhowl Warlock-Knight"**: **black-and-white** Shih Tzu (black #2c2a30, white #f4f0ea).
  - The most magic-heavy: a hooded **tabard and cloak** in deep plum (#4a2a4a) over light armour.
  - A spell-tome or lantern on the belt, rune-stitched hems.
  - The hood is down or tipped back so the face shows.
  - The magic glows **teal-green ghostlight** (#5ce0c0).
- **Option C, "Grim Snuggle Knight"**: **silver** Shih Tzu (silver-grey #b8b8c4, white #f6f6f8).
  - The bold silhouette: big rounded pauldrons with soft bone motifs, a **tattered-hem cape** in charcoal-violet
    (#34303e), and a chunky gorget.
  - A plush-skull charm (cute, toy-like) on the belt.
  - A crown-like topknot band.
  - The magic glows **moonlight lilac and silver** (#b8a8ff).

## Practical constraints (he's animated)
- He walks, runs, swings a heavy flail one- or two-handed, spins it overhead, and casts with one paw raised.
- **The arms and legs stay visible.** The cape stops above the knees and doesn't trail.
- Armour plates are smooth and rounded. Ear locks and the topknot are chunky volumes. Nothing thin or long.

## Sheet requirements
One landscape sheet per option, laid out like sheet-B:
- a **front, 3/4, side and back A-pose turnaround** on a common ground line;
- **head close-ups** (front and 3/4) with 3 expressions: **solemn** ("the darkness…"), **happy** (eyes closed in a smile)
  and **flustered** (cheeks pink, a sweat drop);
- two **action poses**: a **flail spin** overhead, and a **casting** pose with a curse sigil;
- **callouts**: the Toy Flail, and the dark-magic spell looks;
- hex swatches;
- a light, flat background.

## Done when
- 3 sheets, `concepts/option-A.png`, `option-B.png` and `option-C.png`, plus `concepts/notes.md`.
- Each reads unmistakably as a **cute male Shih Tzu dark knight**: the topknot, the chrysanthemum face, the drapey ears,
  the flail, the dark magic. Each also reads as the same toy line as the cast, and differs from Chewy, Poe and Moka.
- The four views agree well enough to model from.
