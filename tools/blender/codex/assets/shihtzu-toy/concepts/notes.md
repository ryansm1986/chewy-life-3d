# Shih Tzu Knight — concept options

Three landscape model sheets for Chewy Life 3D, generated with the built-in image-generation tool using the supplied cast sheets as style references. Final generation prompts are recorded in generation-prompts.md.

## Shared modelling targets

- Male biped Shih Tzu, approximately 2.4 heads tall.
- Height: 1.00 m from ground to head crown, excluding the topknot; topknot target adds approximately 0.12 m. Head-to-remaining-body height ratio: 1:1.4 (about 0.42 m head and 0.58 m remaining body). These are design targets, not measurements taken from the painted sheets.
- Neutral turnaround: front, three-quarter, true side and back, same scale and common ground line; arms approximately 30° out, separated feet and empty closed mitten paws.
- Large smooth ear locks, chrysanthemum cheek puffs, short moustache and beard; keep all eye rims visible. Iris bottoms should be at or above nose top.
- Cape hems stop above the knees. Preserve exposed limbs, clearance around shoulders, and room for one- and two-handed flail animation.
- Plumed tail attaches at the sacrum and curls upward over the rump; the side view describes its curl. Carry the same connected tail volume through the back view.
- Separate Toy Flail: chew-bone handle, thick braided rope and soft rubber-nub ball. Approximately 1.00 m total straightened reach. The weapon appears in the action and prop panels, not the neutral turnaround.
- Each sheet includes two head closeups, solemn/happy/flustered expressions, overhead flail spin, curse casting, separate flail diagram, three spell looks and eight base-colour swatches.
- Use the listed hex values as material targets; lighting, blush softness and translucent magic introduce rendered shades.

## Option A — Midnight Paladin

File: option-A.png

Idea: A gold-and-cream little moon paladin who treats his violet cape and midnight order with earnest dignity.

Proportions: 2.4 heads tall; head-to-remaining-body ratio 1:1.4; 1.00 m to head crown plus approximately 0.12 m topknot. Compact balanced plate-armour silhouette, long gold drapey ear locks and broad cream beard.

Palette:
- Cream fur / bone toy: #F2E2C4
- Gold coat: #D9A35A
- Midnight-blue armour: #2A3058
- Silver trim / circlet: #C8CCD8
- Violet cape / ribbon / magic: #7A4AC8
- Amber eyes: #D89537
- Nose / dark line: #403646
- Blush: #E9A4AC

Distinctive features: Rounded blue plate armour, silver crescent circlet with bat-wing side flares, violet topknot ribbon and short violet cape with a crescent-paw crest. More formal and balanced than B, less broad and heavy than C. Warm coat contrasts with cool armour.

Prop and spells: Violet braided-rope flail with midnight-blue rubber ball; dripping violet paw curse, ghost bones and gloom ring.

Modelling emphasis: Build the circlet as an open band leaving the face and topknot visible. Treat ear and beard separations as broad sculpted lobes, not hair strands. Keep the hip plates short and clear of the thighs.

## Option B — Gloomhowl Warlock-Knight

File: option-B.png

Idea: A black-and-white, book-carrying knight wrapped in plum cloth, solemnly tending friendly teal ghost magic.

Proportions: 2.4 heads tall; head-to-remaining-body ratio 1:1.4; 1.00 m to head crown plus approximately 0.12 m topknot. Cloth-led outline with broad drooping black ear locks, white blaze/muzzle/beard and a white topknot.

Palette:
- Black fur / nose: #2C2A30
- White fur / bone toy: #F4F0EA
- Deep-plum tabard / cloak / cord: #4A2A4A
- Light charcoal armour: #34303E
- Silver trim: #C8CCD8
- Teal-green ghostlight: #5CE0C0
- Amber eyes: #C88A3A
- Blush: #E9A4AC

Distinctive features: Hood fully down behind the shoulders, split plum tabard over light armour, large hem motifs, and a chunky closed spell-tome on the wearer's left hip (viewer right in front view). Magic and cloth dominate rather than A's blue plates or C's massive shoulders. Black-and-white Shih Tzu face and draping ears distinguish him from Poe's pug.

Prop and spells: Plum braided-rope flail with teal-lit rubber ball; dripping paw curse, friendly floppy-eared spirit pup and teal gloom ring.

Modelling emphasis: The folded hood belongs behind the neck, never above the eyes. Keep the tome compact and rigid at the hip. Final art was refined to remove excessive fur grooves and use large smooth ear, muzzle and tail volumes.

## Option C — Grim Snuggle Knight

File: option-C.png

Idea: A silver-coated, broad-shouldered cuddle knight with chew-bone armour motifs and a tiny plush-skull good-luck charm.

Proportions: 2.4 heads tall; head-to-remaining-body ratio 1:1.4; 1.00 m to head crown plus approximately 0.12 m topknot. Widest shoulder silhouette of the three, with jaw-length rounded ear locks clearing the large pauldrons.

Palette:
- Silver-grey fur / trim / crown band: #B8B8C4
- White markings / bone motifs / toy: #F6F6F8
- Muted charcoal-purple armour: #4D495A
- Charcoal-violet cape: #34303E
- Moonlight-lilac magic / crest: #B8A8FF
- Amber eyes: #C88A3A
- Nose / dark line: #25232E
- Blush: #E9A4AC

Distinctive features: Oversized rounded bone pauldrons, thick low gorget, crown-like rounded topknot band, front-centre plush-skull belt charm and a short scalloped cape. Broad protective shape contrasts with A's balanced paladin and B's cloth-led warlock. The silver coat and lilac magic establish a moonlit identity.

Prop and spells: Silver/lilac braided-rope flail with charcoal-purple rubber ball and blunt pale nubs; lilac paw curse, ghost bones and moonlight gloom ring.

Modelling emphasis: Keep the gorget below the beard, shoulder caps round, and cape notches shallow and blunt. Preserve shoulder rotation clearance. Final art was refined to replace strand-like coat texture with smooth sculpted masses.

## Delivery check

All three final sheets include the requested turnaround, closeups, three expressions, two action poses, separate toy flail, three spell looks and hex palette. No major requested panel was omitted. These are concept blueprints; use the neutral turnaround as the construction reference and the action drawings as movement and personality guidance.
