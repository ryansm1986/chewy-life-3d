# Model brief: oni-chef (enemy series EN-1, the Oni Kitchen's boss)

The same contract and one-piece note as `brief.md` (a single `body` object, origin at the ground centre, vertex colours
only, `toon` material). Build **Oni Chef Gorobei** from the **callout on the approved sheet** (`refs/sheet.png`: the Oni
Chef front and 3/4), on top of your approved oni-imp (the same head, face, horns and build style).

## What it is
The boss of the Oni Kitchen: a big red oni chef who waddles, lobs fire pots and swings his cleaver. The game scales the
model **×2.4** (about **4.3 m** in the game), so build it about **1.8 units** tall to the top of the toque; one rigid piece
(the game waddles, squashes and tilts it whole).

## Look (follow the sheet's callout)
- A big red oni (skin **#ff5a4a**), cream horns, the oni-imp's face style with a grand dark **moustache** (#30232b) and a
  stern-proud chef look.
- A towering white **toque** (puffy top, a band; whites #fff9ef, not pure white: the game's bloom would glare) and a white
  **apron** over a round belly, the apron's tie at the back.
- A **cleaver** in one hand (a broad, rounded-corner blade, steel #b8bcc8 with a darker spine, a wooden handle) and a
  **ladle** in the other (a round bowl, a long handle). Chunky and toy-like: rounded edges, ≥ 4 cm thick.
- His tiger-stripe loincloth may peek out under the apron (as the callout).

## Budget and file
≤ 7,000 triangles. One GLB: `D:/projects/chewy-life-3d/public/models/oni-chef.glb`.

## Done when
Matches the callout (front and 3/4); reads at the 45° game camera at 12 m and 24 m (at ×2.4): the toque, the face and
moustache, the apron, the cleaver and ladle clear; one `body` object.
