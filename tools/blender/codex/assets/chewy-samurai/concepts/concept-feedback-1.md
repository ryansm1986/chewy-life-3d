# Round 2: a black-based outfit

The owner likes **Option B, "Haori Captain"** (the shapes and the layering), but wants a **black-based** outfit instead of red,
white and blue. Make **three new sheets** on Option B's exact design: the same haori cut and length, kimono, hakama,
cord, saya and arm wraps, and the same layout and panels. **Only the colours and crest treatment change.** Leave A, B
and C untouched.

**Chewy himself doesn't change**, in every sheet: the face, ears, coat #6B3E2B / #9A5A3A, the white blaze #F4ECE0, the
amber eyes and the proportions, exactly as sheet-B.

## Readability (important)
He's a **chocolate-brown** dog. Black cloth next to dark-brown fur must still separate clearly at the game camera (45°
down, about 20 m):
- keep a clear light edge between fur and black cloth (the cream kimono collar at the neck, the cream arm wraps), as Option B
  does;
- give the black cloth a soft cool sheen with visible fold highlights, so it reads as black fabric, not a flat silhouette;
- keep the crests large and crisp.

In each sheet's notes, say how it reads at thumbnail size.
