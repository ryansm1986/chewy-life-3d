# Chewy samurai costume concepts

The original three landscape 1536 x 1024 PNG sheets were generated with the built-in image-generation tool, using refs/sheet-B.png as the strict identity and wardrobe reference. The supplied game views, Moka sheet and cast lineup were studied for context. Round 2 adds D, E and F as colour variants of Option B, documented below. No Blender, browser, game or modelling code was used.

## Shared identity and proportions

Every option targets the approved Toybox Chewy: 1.00 m tall, approximately 2.4 head lengths from ground to crown, excluding ear tips. Head height is approximately 0.417 m; the remaining height beneath the head is approximately 0.583 m, giving a head-to-rest-of-body height ratio of 1:1.4. These are reference proportions, not new mesh measurements.

Keep the existing Chewy head, face, coat, ears, markings, paws, feet and tail. Use sheet-B and the existing Blender mesh as the anatomical authority; these images specify costumes. Clothing can obscure part of the chest blaze but must never replace or recolour it. Forearm wraps are retained in all three.

Shared palette:
- Fur: #6B3E2B
- Light fur: #9A5A3A
- White chin/chest blaze, cream wraps and bone: #F4ECE0
- Amber eyes: #E0A040
- Nose: #6A3A2A
- Pink-brown inner ears, interpreted supplementary colour: #A86450
- Signature red, grip wrap and tennis ball: #D8402E
- Navy/indigo and saya: #243A6A
- Gold fittings: #E8B84A

Shared sword specification: one separate gently curved bone blade, smoothly rounded tip, red-wrapped grip, cream double-lobed bone-knob pommel and round gold tsuba with paw-shaped through-holes. Navy saya has a smooth gold distal cap. In turnarounds it is tucked in the obi at Chewy's anatomical LEFT hip: viewer-right in front, viewer-left in back. The profile faces left and exposes that hip. Turnaround paws are empty closed mittens. The two-handed action has a drawn sword and an empty hip saya; iai shows the sword still at the hip. The red tennis ball is a separate callout with cream tennis seams.

## Review and limits

All sheets contain front, three-quarter, left-side and back A-pose turnarounds on a shared ground line; front/three-quarter head portraits; kind, battle-focus and goofy expressions; iai and two-handed action callouts; weapon/saya and red-ball callouts; hex swatches.

Weapon corrections were generated before delivery: removed duplicate hip hilts from drawn-sword actions, rounded the captain blade tip, corrected captain grip pommels, and restored gold scabbard end caps.

These are generated concept drawings, so exact pixel-for-pixel identity, orthographic mesh accuracy and identical pleat/cord placement across all views cannot be guaranteed. The base mesh must remain unchanged. Minor cord knots, pleat spacing, sheath curvature and perspective foreshortening vary; resolve those with the four main turnarounds, not the smaller action thumbnails. The enlarged paw-guard callout on B/C is the clearest guide to actual cut-out geometry; shadows in smaller guards can look like dark decoration.

Every required content category is represented. Actual arm clearance, 45-degree game-camera visibility, cloth deformation, draw reach and rolling clearance are design intentions shown in the poses, not rig or runtime tests. No animation or game validation was performed, as requested.

The full prompt set and localized revision prompts are recorded in generation-prompts.md. Originals remain under C:/Users/there/.codex/generated_images/01a10db2-18ba-7cb2-8544-d303fdfec950/.

## Round 2 — black-based Haori Captain variants

Files: [option-D.png](option-D.png), [option-E.png](option-E.png), [option-F.png](option-F.png). Each is a 1536 x 1024 PNG edited with the built-in image-generation tool from Option B; refs/sheet-B.png supplied the identity reference. Shared design: B's short open haori, collar band, cream arm wraps, overlapping kimono, obi, separate mid-shin hakama legs, compact tie, left-hip saya, bare brown feet and exposed tail. The same panel positions, A-pose turnarounds, head close-ups, kind / battle-focus / goofy portraits, iai and two-handed stances, drawn Bone Katana, tsuba detail, bone pommel detail and tennis ball are retained. The standalone saya callout now includes a seated sword to show the requested sheathed prop; the two-handed character has an empty hip saya.

Chewy's identity palette and proportions remain the specification: coat #6B3E2B / #9A5A3A; blaze #F4ECE0; eyes #E0A040; nose #6A3A2A; inner ears #A86450; 1.00 m and 2.4 heads. The fur, face, ears, paws and tail were visually compared with B. These remain raster concepts rather than measurements of the existing mesh.

Black cloth in all three uses broad soft cool-neutral fold highlights. Retain the cream neck edge and forearm wraps as bright separation shapes. Keep the two chest paw mon and large back paw mon crisp, without shrinking them when modelling.
