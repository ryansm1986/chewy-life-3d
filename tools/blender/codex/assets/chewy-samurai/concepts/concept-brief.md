# Concept brief: chewy-samurai-concept (Toybox Chibi, re-dress)

## The character
**Chewy** is the game's star and first playable hero: a chocolate pup with a bone sword, who also throws a red tennis ball.
The owner wants his moves and style to become **samurai**: upright, formal and disciplined, with quick-draw cuts, stances
and two-handed swings. The game's other heroes are Moka (a mage) and a new **Pug Ninja**: swift, low, hidden, with small
blades and smoke. Chewy should read as the ninja's opposite.

**This is a re-dress, not a new character.** The attached `sheet-B.png` is Chewy's approved Toybox model sheet, and
`chewy-toy-game-crop.png`, `chewy-game-front.png` and `chewy-game-side.png` show him in the game. **Keep everything about
him except the clothes:**
- the head, face, eyes, brows, nose and smile, exactly as on sheet-B;
- the ears, coat and markings;
- the proportions (about 1.0 m, 2.4 heads tall), the mitten paws, the feet and the tail.

Draw him from sheet-B and **only change the outfit and the sword**. Deliver **3 samurai outfit options**. The owner picks
one, and it's then modelled onto his existing Blender model.

## Style (fixed: sheet-B)
- The Toybox Chibi vinyl-toy look of sheet-B, `moka-sheet.png` and `cast-lineup.png`: soft, matte, smooth surfaces with
  gentle painted shading; clothes as a few big sculpted, clean shapes with crisp colour borders; no fine fabric detail.
- It must read from the game camera (45° down, about 20 m away), with a strong silhouette and big shapes. **No headgear
  may hide the eyes from 45° above.**

## Identity (every option must keep these)
- **Coat** (from sheet-B):
  - fur #6B3E2B, light fur #9A5A3A;
  - the white chin and chest blaze #F4ECE0;
  - amber eyes #E0A040;
  - nose #6A3A2A;
  - folded ears with pink-brown insides;
  - a short tail.
- **Red is his signature accent** (#D8402E, today's scarf and sash). Every option keeps a clear red element: a scarf, sash,
  tasuki cords or sword cord.
- **Navy or indigo** (#243A6A) can stay as a main colour, but one option should try a different main colour.
- **Not the ninja's colours**: avoid charcoal with plum, cream with icy blue, or moss green as the main colours.
- **The Bone Katana**: his sword becomes samurai-style. It's a **separate game prop**, held in the right paw or both paws in
  play, so:
  - show it only as a callout: a long, gently curved **bone blade** (bone-coloured, rounded tip, bone-knob pommel), a
    round **tsuba** with paw-print cut-outs, and a **red-wrapped grip**;
  - draw the turnaround with the sword **sheathed in its saya at the left hip**, the saya tucked in the obi (that part is
    costume);
  - the paws are soft closed mittens.
- **The red tennis ball stays** (his Fetch tree). Show it as a small callout, or carried in a small pouch on the obi.
- **Personality**: kind, brave and loyal, a goofy pup at heart who becomes very focused in a fight.

## The three options (the same Chewy, three samurai outfits)
- **Option A, "Young Samurai"**: the faithful samurai update of today's look.
  - A navy kimono top (#243A6A).
  - Charcoal **hakama** to mid-shin (#2E2A36).
  - Red **tasuki cords** crossed in an X on the back to tie up the sleeves, and a red obi.
  - Straw waraji sandals over cream tabi.
  - The Bone Katana's saya at the left hip.
- **Option B, "Haori Captain"**: the formal look.
  - A short **haori jacket** in a bold main colour (try a deep red #B83A2E, or a warm persimmon), with a big white **paw-print
    family crest (mon)** on the back and smaller ones on the chest.
  - A cream kimono underneath.
  - Indigo hakama.
  - A red-and-white cord tying the haori.
  - Optional: a little dog-ear-shaped **topknot tuft** of fur on the crown.
- **Option C, "Armoured Samurai"**: the bold silhouette.
  - **Light toy armour** over the kimono and hakama:
    - chunky lacquered **sode** shoulder plates and a rounded **dō** chest plate in navy and red with gold lacing (#E8B84A);
    - simple, smooth plates, no tiny scales.
  - Headgear: either a small **kabuto** helmet with holes for the folded ears and a golden **bone-shaped crest (maedate)**,
    or a red **hachimaki** headband with a gold bone plate if a helmet would hide the face.
  - The arms must swing freely under the shoulder plates.

## Practical constraints (he's animated in the game)
- He walks, runs, dashes and rolls. He swings the sword one- and two-handed, draws it from the hip, throws a ball with one
  arm, and barks or howls with his head up.
- **The hakama is split per leg**: wide, pleated trouser legs that follow each leg, ending mid-shin so the feet stay
  clearly visible. It's never a skirt, and never touches the ground.
- Nothing long trails: sleeves are tied back or short, and cords and tails are short and chunky.
- **The arms and legs stay visible**: chunky toy limbs, as now. Shoulder pieces must not block the arm swing.
- Any headgear sits **firmly on the head** as one rigid shape, with the folded ears visible.

## Sheet requirements
One landscape sheet per option, laid out **like sheet-B**:
- an **A-pose turnaround** in **front, 3/4, side and back** views, consistent with each other, on a common ground line;
- a **head close-up**, front and 3/4, with **3 expressions**:
  - **kind** (sheet-B's smile);
  - **battle focus** (determined brows, steady eyes, mouth closed);
  - **goofy** (tongue out, as on sheet-B);
- two **action pose** callouts:
  - an **iai quick-draw stance**: crouched low, one paw on the hilt at the hip, about to draw;
  - a **two-handed stance**: the Bone Katana raised in both paws;
- the **Bone Katana** (drawn and in its saya) and the **red tennis ball** as small callouts;
- hex swatches;
- a light, flat, evenly lit background.

## Done when
- 3 sheets, `concepts/option-A.png`, `option-B.png` and `option-C.png`, plus `concepts/notes.md` (each option's idea and
  palette, and anything that was hard to keep consistent).
- Each one is **unmistakably the same Chewy as sheet-B** (the face, ears, coat, blaze and proportions unchanged) **dressed
  as a samurai**. Each reads as the same toy line, and the three outfits are clearly different.
- The four views agree well enough to model from. The hakama legs, the saya at the hip and any shoulder pieces or headgear
  must be clear in the side and back views.
