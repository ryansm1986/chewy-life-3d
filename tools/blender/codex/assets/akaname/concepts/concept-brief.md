# Concept brief: akaname-concept (Pawhaven enemy series, Onsen Caverns)

The series so far: the owner approved `approved-series-example-takenoko.png` (Bamboo Grove) and
`approved-series-example-kuri.png` (Momiji Hollow). Match their style, finish and sheet layout.

## The monster
**Akaname**, the bath-licking yōkai who haunts old bathhouses and licks the tubs clean, the **grabber** of Onsen Caverns
(the zone dungeon under Yukimi Onsen). Packs of 2–4. About **1.0 m tall**, chubby.

What it does in a fight (design for this):
- **Lick** (mid range): it squats, sucks its tongue in, then **lashes its long pink tongue** out up to 6.5 m; caught,
  you're slobbered and **yanked** toward it. Afterwards it pants, tongue hanging out.
- **Steam** (close): it puffs up, steam whistling out of its ears, then bursts in a cloud of bath steam.
- It is warm-blooded among the snow folk: steam curls off its head.

Identity to keep (every option):
- A chubby little **red imp** (skin #e8463e, light #ff7a64, belly #ffb49a), a big round head with a shock of **black
  hair** (#24182a) and two nub **horns**.
- A white **tenugui** towel folded on top of its head (#fbf8f2 with a blue stripe #3a6ec0).
- **Huge eyes**, a wide grin with a long **pink tongue** lolling out, blush.
- A round belly, stubby clawed feet, and a **tawashi** scrub brush in one fist.
- Personality: a giggly, slobbery, mischievous bathhouse gremlin.

Moving parts (draw them as separate pieces):
- the **body** (head, hair, horns, towel, belly, feet: one rigid piece);
- the **tongue** (its own piece from the mouth: it stretches out far; draw it at rest lolling out, and as a callout fully
  lashed out as a long ribbon);
- the **two arms**, each on its own shoulder joint (one holds the tawashi).

Signature attack pose callout: the **lick**: the tongue lashing out long and wavy, the imp leaning forward, eyes squeezed.

Variant strip (same model, colourways):
1. base: as above;
2. **Yuzu Akaname**: orange-red skin #f05a32, a **blue** towel #5a8ad8, a little **yuzu** fruit on its towel;
3. **Elder Akaname**: deep crimson #b42a3a, grey hair #a8a4ae, **white brows and beard**, a red-striped **hachimaki**
   headband.

## The three options (the same style, genuinely different designs)
- **Option A, "Bathhouse Imp"**: the current idea perfected: a round chubby red imp, the folded towel on its head, the
  tongue lolling, the scrub brush.
- **Option B, "Bath Boss"**: a little bathhouse attendant: a short happi coat and a towel round the neck, a wooden bath
  bucket (oke) under one arm, a cheeky grin.
- **Option C, "Steam Bun"**: the most chibi: a round steamed-bun body that's nearly all head, tiny horns, a tiny towel,
  steam curls, tiny limbs; the cutest.
