# Concept brief: kuri-concept (Pawhaven enemy series, Momiji Hollow)

The series so far: the owner approved `approved-series-example-takenoko.png` (Option A) and
`approved-series-example-kodama.png` (Option B) for the first region. Match their style, finish and sheet layout.

## The monster
**Kuri**, a chestnut sprite, the common melee enemy of Momiji Hollow (the autumn maple region). Packs of 8–16. About
**0.8 m tall**, a round ball about 0.85 m across when shut.

What it does in a fight (design for this):
- It hops about with its **spiky burr open** like a collar behind its face.
- It **curls up**: the burr's **two halves swing shut** round the nut into a spiky ball, and it **rolls** at you fast.
- Then it uncurls, dizzy, and snaps at you.

Identity to keep (every option):
- A glossy **chestnut nut** (#9a4c22, a pale cream #f0dcb0 base patch where it sat in the burr, a little pointed tip on
  top) with the face on the nut.
- The **burr**: a spiky green husk (#8cc43e, pale tips #eef6a8) in **two halves hinged at the back**, opening forward like
  a clam into a spiky V collar with a cream lining (#f4e6c0) facing forward. Spikes are **toy-like**: short, chunky,
  rounded cones, never needle-thin.
- Stubby little feet; maybe tiny nub hands.
- Personality: a feisty, puffed-up little brawler (current look: angry brows, a tiny fang, blush).

Moving parts (draw them as separate pieces): the **nut/body** (one rigid piece with the face and feet) and the **two
burr halves** (left and right, each hinged on a vertical axis at the back of the nut). Show the burr **open** in the
turnaround, and add a small callout of the **shut ball** (the halves closed round the nut, only spikes showing).

Signature attack pose callout: the **roll**: the shut spiky ball tumbling with speed lines and a dust puff.

Variant strip (same model, colourways):
1. base: burr #8cc43e / tips #eef6a8, nut #9a4c22;
2. **Roasted Kuri** (fire): burr #6a4428 with glowing orange tips #ffae3a, nut #6a3216, lining #b88a58, a little ember glow
   in the burr's seams;
3. **Momiji Kuri**: burr #c2d45a with red-orange tips #ff6a3a, nut #a85a2a, plus one red maple leaf (#e8482e) stuck on top.

## The three options (the same style, genuinely different designs)
- **Option A, "Spiky Bruiser"**: the current idea perfected: a round glossy nut-face, the burr halves open behind it like
  a big spiky collar or a lion's mane, chunky feet.
- **Option B, "Burr Knight"**: the burr worn like **armour**: the halves shaped like a little helmet-and-cape that close
  into the ball; the nut more egg-shaped and upright, a determined knightly face.
- **Option C, "Chestnut Bub"**: the most chibi: the nut is a big round head that's nearly all face, the burr halves smaller
  and rounder like two spiky earmuffs or wings at its sides, tiny legs; the cutest.
