# Asset brief: kuma-toy (the Toybox Kuma, phase 1: the model)

Read `../villager-toy-common.md` first: the shared technical rules, the lessons and the process rules.

## Who
**Kuma**, Blossom Hollow's baker: a big, gentle brown bear who "bakes the fluffiest melon-pan on the island". Warm, shy,
a little bashful. He's the chubbiest villager (the game builds him 5% bigger and 5% chubbier than the others).
The blueprint is `refs/sheet.png` ("KUMA · GENTLE BAKER"), approved by the owner.

## Must keep (from the sheet)
- **Fur** #A86A44 all over, soft and smooth; **muzzle and the round ear insides** #E8C49A; a small round stub tail
  (back view).
- **Face**: big dark-brown eyes (iris #4B2C20, pupil #2D1B16) with the thick upper lash line and wing; soft arched brows;
  a wide oval light muzzle with a dark rounded-triangle nose #2D1B16 and a small "w" smile; blush ovals #EAA095.
- **Ears**: round, set at the top corners of the head and partly overlapped by the hat band; thick discs, not flat.
- **Chef's toque** #FFFFFF: a puffy cloud top of 4–5 smooth lobes on a straight band, about 0.25 m tall on top of the head
  (the sheet: 1.15 m without it, 1.40 m with it). Judge it from the 45° camera: it must read as a soft cloud, not a
  flat cap.
- **Green neckerchief** #7CC45A tied at the front, with a round knot and two short rounded ends (thick, not ribbons).
- **The apron** #FFF6E8 with a few soft flour dabs (#FFFFFF): a bib front from the chest to above the knees, rounded
  corners, a waist tie that wraps to the back with a flat rounded buckle (back view). The belly fur shows at the sides.
- **Body**: round and chubby, short thick arms with mitten paws (a light pad tint optional), short legs, broad rounded
  feet with toe lines.

## The director's rough measurements (cross-check yours, Step 0)
On HEAD FRONT (W = head width at the cheeks, ears excluded; y measured down from the hat band's lower edge):
- eyes: centres at x = ±0.23 W, about 0.33 W above the chin; opening about 0.21 W wide × 0.22 W tall;
- muzzle: an oval about 0.37 W wide × 0.25 W tall, its centre about 0.14 W above the chin;
- nose: about 0.11 W wide, at the top of the muzzle; mouth about 0.15 W wide, just under it;
- blush: about 0.12 × 0.07 W at x = ±0.36 W, level with the muzzle top;
- ears: discs about 0.28 W across, centred at x = ±0.36 W at the hat-band height.
- Total height 1.15 m to the crown (under the hat), 1.40 m with the hat.

neckerchief reading well in `game.png` from both 45° yaws, ≤ 30k triangles, one atlas, `joints.json`.
