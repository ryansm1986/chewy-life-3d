## Fixes
1. **The head shape (the main one).** Yours is a bell: it narrows toward the top (the temples slope in), and from the
   portrait the head reads as a cone under the hat. The sheet's head is a soft squircle, nearly as wide at the top as
   at the cheeks, with rounded corners where the ears sit. Measured on the sheet's HEAD FRONT (W = the cheek width):
   - width at 0.60 W above the chin: about **0.86 W** (yours is about 0.66 W);
   - width at 0.75 W above the chin (just under the band): about **0.78 W**;
   - the cheeks stay as they are (your lower widths are within 2.5%).
   Widen the upper head to these numbers, keep the face convex and the crown under the band, and move the ears out with
   the corners so they stay centred on them (same size, x about ±0.40 W).
2. **The muzzle is a lump.** In the 3/4 views and the portrait it projects like a snout with a dark underside. On the
   sheet it's a nearly flat painted oval with only a gentle swell. Bring its relief down to at most **0.03 W** proud of
   the face, blend it smoothly into the cheeks and the chin, and remove the dark shading under it. The nose stays where
   it is, on top.
3. **The apron.** In the side view it's a flat vertical slab standing off the belly, and from the front it's a stiff
   rectangle. On the sheet it's soft cloth that **wraps the round belly**: in profile it follows the belly's curve
   (at most 0.02 m off the fur), the skirt part hangs from the widest point of the belly with a gently curved hem, and
   its sides curve back round the body. Keep the bib, the arch where the belly shows, the waist tie and the flour dabs,
   with a cloth thickness of about 0.015–0.02 m and rounded edges.
4. **The neckerchief.** The band is a flat glossy ribbon and the knot has a deep vertical crease (it reads as a seam).
   Make the band a soft rolled cloth (thicker at the front, about 0.05 m, thinning toward the back), and the knot one
   smooth round knot with a shallow fold at most. The two leaf ends are good.
5. **Fur colour.** In the portrait the forehead and cheeks go orange (around #D08850). Keep the palette #A86A44: lower
   the warmth and brightness of the painted highlight so the fur reads as the sheet's mid brown, with light only as a
   soft value change.
