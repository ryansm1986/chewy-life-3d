# Concept brief: dustbunny-concept (Pawhaven enemy series, the Burrow)

The series so far: the owner approved `approved-series-example-takenoko.png` (Bamboo Grove) and
`approved-series-example-kuri.png` (Momiji Hollow). Match their style, finish and sheet layout.

The Burrow is the game's first dungeon: four little dens under the village (the Mossy Burrow, the Fox Shrine, the Oni
Kitchen, the Moon Sanctum). Its monsters are the first enemies a young player meets, so they are the simplest and
friendliest-looking of the series.

**This kind is one rigid piece** (the game bobs, squashes, tilts and spins the whole model; nothing on it moves on its
own), so design it as one solid toy figure: no separate swinging parts needed.

## The monster
**Dust Bunny**, a fluffy ball of dust that scurries in swarms, the fast fodder of the Burrow. Packs of 8–16. About
**0.65 m tall**, a fluffy ball with ears.

What it does in a fight: it **scurries** fast and nips at you in little swarms.

Identity to keep (every option): a round **fluff ball** made of chunky soft puffs (lavender grey #d0c8dc), two floppy
**bunny ears** of fluff, big eyes peeking out of the fluff, a tiny nose, a bit of cheeky attitude. Fluff is **chunky
rounded puffs**, never hair strands.

Variant strip (same model, colourways): 1. lavender grey #d0c8dc; 2. blue grey #c4ccd8; 3. warm grey #dccabc.

## The three options (the same style, genuinely different designs)
- **Option A, "Fluff Ball"**: the current idea perfected: a cluster of puffs with two fluff ears and two big eyes.
- **Option B, "Lint Rascal"**: a lop-eared dust bunny with a long fluffy tail-trail of dust puffs and tiny feet.
- **Option C, "Puff Bub"**: the most chibi: one big round puff with a huge face and two tiny ears; the cutest.
