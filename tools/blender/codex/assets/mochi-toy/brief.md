# Asset brief: mochi-toy (the Toybox Mochi, phase 1: the model)

Read `../villager-toy-common.md` first: the shared technical rules, the lessons and the process rules.

## Who
**Mochi**, Blossom Hollow's painter: "a dreamy calico who paints the sunrise every morning". Soft, dreamy, gentle.
The blueprint is `refs/sheet.png` ("MOCHI · SUNRISE DREAMER"), approved by the owner.

## Must keep (from the sheet)
- **The head is a soft mochi shape**: much wider than tall (about 1.3 : 1), a flat-ish top rounding down to full cheeks.
  It's her signature, so keep it wide, but the face must still wrap a rounded surface (convex in profile).
- **Fur** #FFF4EA with **orange calico patches** #F4A860, clean rounded shapes:
  - one over her right eye (the viewer's left), running from the crown down past the eye's outer corner, the eye itself
    on cream;
  - the whole right ear orange (the left ear cream with an orange rim, as on the sheet);
  - a big rounded patch on the back of the head;
  - the tail's tip.
- **Ears**: triangular cat ears with rounded tips, thick, inner ears #FFB0C0.
- **Face**: big green eyes (iris #71B86B) with the lash line and wing; soft brows; a tiny nose #CB7F86 and a small "w"
  mouth; blush #F5A0AD. No whiskers.
- **Kimono top** #FF9EC0 with a white crossed collar and wide short sleeves; **obi** #FFD24A with a flat bow knot at the
  back; a **paintbrush** tucked in the obi at her left front (handle #A87550, bristles #6B4A38, thick and simple).
- **Short skirt** #6A4A6A, an A-line to above the knees.
- **Tail**: a long cream tail with an orange tip, curving up and out behind (a separate piece with bend loops, thick).
- **Body**: small and round, mitten paws, rounded feet with toe lines.

## The director's rough measurements (cross-check yours, Step 0)
On HEAD FRONT (W = head width at the cheeks, ears excluded):
- head height (crown to chin, ears excluded) about 0.77 W;
- eyes: centres at x = ±0.21 W, about 0.33 W above the chin; opening about 0.23 W wide × 0.25 W tall;
- nose about 0.05 W wide just under the eye line; mouth about 0.10 W;
- blush about 0.13 × 0.08 W at x = ±0.31 W;
- ears: triangles with a base about 0.33 W wide, their tips about 0.15 W above the crown.
- Total height 1.08 m.

## Done when
As in the shared brief: within about 5% of your measured targets, appealing in `portrait.png`, the wide head, the
patches and the tail reading well in `game.png` from both 45° yaws, ≤ 30k triangles, one atlas, `joints.json`.
