# Concept brief: kappa-concept (Pawhaven enemy series, Shiokaze Tidepools)

The series so far: the owner approved `approved-series-example-takenoko.png` (Bamboo Grove) and
`approved-series-example-kuri.png` (Momiji Hollow). Match their style, finish and sheet layout.

## The monster
**Kappa**, the river imp of Japanese folklore, the common melee enemy of the Shiokaze Tidepools (a sunny beach of rock
pools). Packs of 6–12. About **1.0 m tall**, chubby and round.

What it does in a fight (design for this):
- It waddles about, then does a **lunging grab**: both arms shoot forward together, it hugs you, then splashes you off.
- A **head-flick splash**: it tips its head back, then flicks it forward so the water in its dish sloshes out at you.
- **The water in its head dish is its strength**: the level drops as it gets hurt. When it is low the kappa runs to the
  nearest water, sits in it and refills (heals). So the dish must be big, open and clearly visible from above.

Identity to keep (every option):
- Frog-green skin (#6ec452), a cream belly plastron (#f6e6a0), a **turtle shell** on its back (#4e7a3c with lighter
  scutes #94b858 and a cream rim #e6d69c), a yellow **duck beak** (#ffc53a).
- The **sara**: a shallow white dish (#f6f1e2) on top of its head holding bright water (#5fe0ee), ringed by a mop of
  dark-green **okappa hair** (#2e5a3a) in chunky tufts (the forehead clear so the face reads).
- Webbed hands and paddle feet (#a8d86a).
- Personality: a cheeky, grabby little pond rascal (current look: big eyes, cross brows, blush).

Moving parts (draw them as separate pieces):
- the **body** (torso, belly, shell, legs and feet: one rigid piece);
- the **head** (with the beak, dish and hair), on a visible neck joint: it tips back and flicks forward;
- the **arms**, as one part (both swing forward together from the shoulders to grab);
- the **dish water**: a separate flat water disc sitting in the dish (it shrinks and sinks as the kappa is hurt).

Signature attack pose callout: the **head-flick splash**: head flung forward, a fan of water flying out of the dish.

Variant strip (same model, colourways):
1. base: as above;
2. **Umi Kappa** (sea): skin #5ab8c4, shell #35598a / scutes #6c9cc8, hair #1f3f66, beak #ffb14a, water #a8f4ff, plus a
   little orange **starfish** (#ff8a4a) hitching a ride on its shell;
3. **Kyuri Kappa** (cucumber): skin #a2cc48, shell #7a6434 / scutes #bca060, hair #5a4428, beak #ffd24a, plus a prized
   **cucumber** (#3e8a2a with pale stripes) held in its right hand.

## The three options (the same style, genuinely different designs)
- **Option A, "Pond Rascal"**: the current idea perfected: a chubby round imp, big round head, a wide dish ringed by a
  shaggy hair mop, a domed shell on its back, a big friendly duck beak.
- **Option B, "Little Sumo"**: a kappa sumo wrestler: a squat, heavy, bottom-wide build with a chunky mawashi belt
  (#f6f1e2 with a knot at the back) under its shell, a determined face, short thick arms made for a bear hug.
- **Option C, "Puddle Bub"**: the most chibi: a big head that is nearly all face and dish, a tiny body, the shell worn
  like a little backpack, stubby limbs; the cutest.
