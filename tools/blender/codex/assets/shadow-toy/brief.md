# Asset brief: shadow-toy (the Toybox Shadow model, phase 1: the model)

## What it is
**Shadow**, Chewy's companion: a **Boston terrier on four legs**. The owner picked design **Option A, "Faithful Buddy"**: the attached
`sheet.png` is **the blueprint**, so match it closely. He's the same toy line as the Toybox Chewy (`sheet-B.png`,
`chewy-toy-game-crop.png`) and the Toybox Rosie (`rosie-sheet-A.png`). Match their finish and quality bar.
`shadow-current-crop.png` is his current model, **not this**.

This task has two phases:
- **Phase 1 (now)**: the model, standing on all four legs in a neutral stance (head up, looking forward), until the director
  rates it 9/10 and the owner signs off.
- **Phase 2 (later)**: a quadruped rig.
Build rig-ready, but don't rig yet.

## Technical
- Faces **−Y**, stands on z = 0 (all four paws), centred.
- **≤ 24k triangles**: he's smaller on screen than Chewy. One material, **one 2048² painted atlas**, no fur strands or cards.
- **Rig-ready for a quadruped**:
  - eyeballs as separate spheres;
  - lid and mouth loops;
  - ears as separate pieces with a bend loop at the base;
  - the tail as its own piece;
  - loops at the shoulders, elbows, hips, knees and neck.
  Write `joints.json` with: root, body (barrel centre), neck, head (skull base), jaw, eye_L/R, ear_L/R (+ tips), legFL/FR/BL/BR
  (shoulder and hip), elbows and knees, paws, tail root.
- Output: `public/models/shadow-toy.glb`, with the standard preview `--character`. A sitting pose is NOT required yet
  (sitting comes with the rig).
- Comparisons against the sheet: front, side, back and the head close-ups (`cmp-head.png`, `cmp-side.png`, `cmp-back.png`,
  `cmp-side-head.png`), plus `portrait.png` and both 45° game-camera shots.

## Done when (phase 1)
- The turnaround and head comparisons match the sheet within about 5% on the measured targets, and the side view especially
  (the muzzle is the only projection, the eyes set back, clear legs).
- `portrait.png` looks appealing, in the same finish as the Toybox Chewy and Rosie.
- From the game camera the tuxedo pattern, bat ears and accessory colour read clearly from both yaws.
- ≤ 24k triangles, one atlas, stands on the ground, faces −Y, no preview warnings, and `joints.json` written.
