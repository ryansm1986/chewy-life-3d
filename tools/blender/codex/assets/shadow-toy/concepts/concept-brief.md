# Concept brief: shadow-concept (Toybox Chibi)

## The character
**Shadow** is Chewy's loyal companion: a little **Boston terrier** who follows him everywhere, sniffs around the village, sits when
Chewy stops, barks at monsters and fights beside him in the Burrow. Village lore: he snores like a tiny tractor and once ate three of
Rosie's strawberry mochi. Brave, goofy, food-loving and devoted. He's **a real dog on four legs** (not a biped like Chewy), about
**0.62 m tall** to the top of the head when standing (ears extra), so he reaches about Chewy's hip.

The game now has a **Toybox Chibi** art style:
- the new Chewy: `sheet-B.png` (his approved model sheet) and `chewy-toy-game-crop.png` (him in the game);
- the new Rosie: `rosie-sheet-A.png`.
**Shadow must look like the same toy line**, as if all three were sold together. Deliver **3 design options**. The owner picks
one, and it's then modelled in Blender. `shadow-current-crop.png` is his current game model: keep the identity, not the look.
It has a narrow fox-like face, maroon eyes, long thin ears and stick legs, all of which the new design replaces. There's no photo
of the real Shadow.

## Style (fixed for every option: match sheet-B and Rosie's sheet)
- A vinyl-toy / Pokopia / Animal Crossing feel.
  - **Proportions**: a **big round head**, about 45% of his standing height, wider than tall; a short, chunky barrel body;
    short, stubby, rounded legs; round paw pads; a tiny nub tail.
  - **Surfaces**: soft, matte, smooth surfaces with gentle painted shading. Fur is **clean colour blocks** with at most a
    few sculpted cheek tufts, never strands.
- The **face language of sheet-B**, adapted to a Boston terrier:
  - **big glossy eyes set wide**, facing a little outward the way Bostons' do: a warm-white sclera showing white crescents,
    a **warm amber-brown iris** (it must read against black fur), a large dark pupil, two catchlights and a soft dark lid
    line;
  - a short, broad, **flat-ish muzzle** (Boston, not fox), with soft muzzle cheeks either side of a central crease;
  - a big rounded black nose with a highlight;
  - a happy closed smile line, and an optional tiny tongue tip;
  - pink blush dabs, subtle against the white muzzle.
- **Bat ears**: big, upright, rounded triangles with pink inner ears (#f0a0a8). They're thick toy volumes, never thin flaps.
- Clean colour blocking with crisp borders; a simple palette with hex swatches.
- It must read from the game camera (45° down, about 20 m away): the tuxedo pattern, the big ears and the collar colour are the
  signature.

## Identity (every option must keep these)
- **Tuxedo markings**:
  - **black/dark slate** fur (#34303f; slightly lifted so it isn't pure black);
  - a **white blaze** down the middle of the face;
  - a **white muzzle**;
  - a **white chest and belly**;
  - **white socks** on all four paws.
- **Bat ears** with pink insides.
- **Amber-brown eyes**, big and warm.
- A **blue collar** (#4aa8f0) with a **gold round tag** (#ffd24a).
- Personality: loyal, brave, goofy. Show it in the expressions.

## The three options (the same style, genuinely different designs)
- **Option A, "Faithful Buddy"**: the classic tuxedo Boston.
  - The cleanest update of his identity: the blue collar with a round gold tag.
  - The roundest, cutest proportions.
- **Option B, "Little Ronin"**: Chewy's partner look.
  - The blue collar is swapped for a **red neckerchief knotted at the side** (#d8402e), matching Chewy's scarf.
  - A small gold bell or tag.
  - A tiny navy (#243a6a) wrap or bandana accent.
  - Slightly sportier, more alert proportions, with ears perkier.
- **Option C, "Burrow Scout"**: his adventurer role.
  - A little **harness** (blue, with a gold buckle) carrying a tiny **lantern** or a small bone-shaped canteen on the back.
  - The gold tag kept.
  - A slightly chubbier, goofier body, with one ear that flops a little.

## Sheet requirements
One landscape sheet per option, laid out **like sheet-B**:
- A **turnaround** of him **standing on all four legs** (a neutral stance, head up, looking forward), in **front, 3/4, side and
  back** views, all consistent with each other, on a common ground line.
- A **head close-up**, front and 3/4, with **3 expressions**: happy tongue-out, alert bark (mouth open), sleepy (eyes closed,
  little snore bubble).
- A small **sitting-pose** callout. The game makes him sit a lot, so show how the legs fold.
- Hex swatches, and the signature accessory as a callout.
- A light, flat, evenly lit background.

## Done when
- 3 sheets, `concepts/option-A.png`, `option-B.png` and `option-C.png`, plus `concepts/notes.md`.
- Each one reads unmistakably as **Shadow** (a tuxedo Boston terrier with bat ears and amber eyes) and as the same toy line as
  Chewy and Rosie, and the three are clearly different designs.
- The four views agree well enough to model from; the legs and paws must be clear in the side view.
