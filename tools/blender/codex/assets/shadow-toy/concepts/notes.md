# Shadow — Toybox Chibi concepts

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool using Chewy's approved sheet and in-game crop, Rosie's sheet, and Shadow's current crop as references. Full generation prompts are in `prompts.md`. The old Shadow reference supplies identity; the new Chewy and Rosie supply the toy-line finish.

## Shared proportions and identity

All options are real quadruped Boston terriers. Target standing height is **0.62 m to the head crown**, excluding ears. Target head height is **about 45% of crown height**, approximately **0.28 m**, giving a head-to-remaining-standing-height ratio of **0.45:0.55** (about **1:1.22**, or **2.22 head heights** total). This is a vertical proportion, not a head-to-torso-length measurement. Sheets are visual concept references; these dimensions govern the eventual model.

Keep a broad, flat, short muzzle; a head wider than tall; wide amber eyes with white sclera crescents; thick bat ears; short rounded legs; four white socks; a tiny nub tail; and the white blaze, muzzle, chest and belly. Fur should be smooth matte vinyl with painted color blocks. Do not reproduce faint fuzzy edges visible in some generated markings as strand fur or fuzzy shaders.

Shared palette: fur **#34303F**; markings/sclera **#F4ECE0**; amber iris **#C88732**; nose **#211D28**; pupil **#17141D**; inner ears **#F0A0A8**; blush **#EEA0A5**; gold **#FFD24A**. Hex values are the production palette; rendered highlights and shading are not color samples.

## C — Burrow Scout

**Idea:** a goofy little adventurer with a full belly, one folded ear tip, and a practical toy harness.

**File:** `option-C.png`.

**Proportions:** 0.62 m crown height; approximately 45% head height; broad round head; fullest barrel belly of the three; extra squat rounded legs. Shadow's **anatomical left** ear has a folded tip; the other is upright. The rear-view ear placement was corrected after inspection.

**Palette:** shared palette plus collar/harness/canteen cap **#4AA8F0**, buckle/tag **#FFD24A**, and bone canteen **#F4ECE0**.

**Difference:** the chubbiest body, asymmetrical ear attitude, and blue harness carrying one small bone-shaped canteen. Use one centered dorsal canteen mount, a broad girth strap behind the forelegs, and a chest connection that leaves the white chest visible. The canteen should be a compact horizontal bone volume as shown in the rear and accessory views; reconcile its exact mounting orientation in the model. The callout clarifies the harness underneath. No lantern is included because the brief allows either accessory.

## Inspection and modelling handoff

Each final 1536 × 1024 landscape sheet contains four standing turnaround views on a shared ground line, front and three-quarter head closeups, happy tongue-out / alert bark / sleepy snore-bubble portraits, a sitting pose, an accessory callout, and labelled hex swatches. Near and far paws are readable in the side views, though B's far paws have the tightest overlap. Sitting callouts show straight front legs and folded hind legs.

The rendered sheets were visually inspected. C's original rear view mirrored the folded ear; the final sheet corrects it. Image generation still produces small perspective and marking-edge variations. Use crisp painted borders and the stated dimensions in Blender. C's gear attachment and the rear ear surfaces need ordinary modelling interpretation; these are not measured orthographic CAD drawings.
