# Concept brief: lantern-concept (Pawhaven enemy series, the Burrow)

The series so far: the owner approved `approved-series-example-takenoko.png` (Bamboo Grove) and
`approved-series-example-kuri.png` (Momiji Hollow). Match their style, finish and sheet layout.

The Burrow is the game's first dungeon: four little dens under the village (the Mossy Burrow, the Fox Shrine, the Oni
Kitchen, the Moon Sanctum). Its monsters are the first enemies a young player meets, so they are the simplest and
friendliest-looking of the series.

**This kind is one rigid piece** (the game bobs, squashes, tilts and spins the whole model; nothing on it moves on its
own), so design it as one solid toy figure: no separate swinging parts needed.

## The monster
**Chōchin Ghost** (chōchin-obake), a haunted paper lantern, the fire caster of the Burrow. Packs of 2–6. About
**1.25 m tall**, floating.

What it does in a fight: it **floats** and **spits fireballs** at you.

Identity to keep (every option): a ribbed **paper lantern** body (orange #ff8a4a) with dark lacquer caps top and bottom
(#2c2430), **one big eye**, a long **lolling tongue** (pink) out of a split in the paper. The game makes its warm parts
glow (design the lantern paper itself as the glowing part: bright, warm, clean flat colour).

Variant strip (same model, colourways): 1. orange #ff8a4a; 2. pink #ff6a8a.

## The three options (the same style, genuinely different designs)
- **Option A, "Paper Lantern"**: the current idea perfected: a classic ribbed chōchin with one big eye and the tongue.
- **Option B, "Festival Lantern"**: a round summer-festival lantern with a painted mon, a cheeky grin and two eyes, little paper streamers as arms.
- **Option C, "Lantern Bub"**: the most chibi: a short round lantern that's nearly all eye and tongue; the cutest.
