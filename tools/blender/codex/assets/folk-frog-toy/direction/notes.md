Proportions: 1.05 m from soles to the top of the eye domes, excluding the cap; approximately 2.4 heads tall. Head height about 0.44 m; the body and legs beneath it occupy about 1.4 head heights. Upright pear-shaped torso, broad squircle head and sturdy short legs.

Outfit: peach short-sleeved shirt; moss-green short overalls with a simple bib, broad shoulder bands, two cream buttons and a rear bib; one thick leaf cap between the eye domes. Bare hands and feet.

Main volumes: softly bevelled squircle head; two integrated spherical eye domes; shallow oval cream muzzle; pear-shaped torso; rounded short cylinders for limbs; mitten hands; flattened oval feet with broad toe divisions. The overall bib is a rounded slab, bands are thick rounded strips, and cap is a flattened leaf-shaped ellipsoid with a broad raised central rib.

Difference: the clearest workwear silhouette, with an upright build, separate trouser legs and a leaf crest. Calmer and sturdier than the small schoolkid; less round than the baker's helper.

## Shared modelling guidance

The pale region is a crisp cream face/muzzle patch that continues onto the belly beneath clothing. These are smooth frogs: no fur, ears, tail, thin webbing or mammalian button nose. Nostrils are two tiny dark dimples on a very shallow rounded muzzle. Use thick eyelid rims, large warm irises, dark pupils, two eye catchlights, modest blush and closed smiles. Keep all bands, hems, fingers and toes chunky enough to read from the game camera.

The upper front and three-quarter views share a ground line. The lower side and back views intentionally use a smaller matched scale, as permitted by this brief.
