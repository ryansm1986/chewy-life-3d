# Asset brief: tk-kazemaru-toy (VL-2: the Blender Master Kazemaru, phase 1: the model)

Read `../villager-toy-common.md` first: the shared technical rules, the lessons and the process rules. **Two differences
from it**:
- This is a **zone villager** (Takemori Village, the bamboo zone), not one of Blossom Hollow's seven.
- The sheet `refs/sheet.png` was drawn for this task (2026-11-02) and approved by the director. The owner delegated the
  pick. It is the blueprint: don't redesign, just build it beautifully.
`refs/current-*-portrait.png` (where present) is the procedural kit version in the game now: identity only.

## Who
**Master Kazemaru**, the sensei of Takemori's dojo and Poe's old teacher, an arctic-white fox: calm, wise and sly; he moves without a sound and naps without shame.
The blueprint is `refs/sheet.png` ("Master Kazemaru - Sensei").

## Must keep (from the sheet)
- **Fur** pale lavender-white #E6E2EA; **muzzle and chest** #FFFFFF; **tall pointed ears** #D8D2DE with mauve inner #C8B0C0 and dark slate **tips** #4A4656 (thick volumes, not cards).
- **Eyes** golden #C8A040; nose #3A3442; blush #E8B0B8.
- A **red headband** #D84A3A, a thick band with a knot at the back and two short, thick, rounded tails (match the side and back views).
- A **navy gi** #34405E with a white collar line, a **black belt** #1E1E28 tied at the front in a rounded knot with two short ends, **hakama trousers** #232838.
- The **big fluffy fox tail** with a white tip: one smooth swelling volume, weighted later to the tail bones.

## Sizes
- Total height **1.10 m** (the sheet's note). Villagers stand about 1.0–1.15 m to the crown.
- Measure everything else on the sheet in Step 0 (`measure.md`, fractions of the head width W). The director checks your
  table against the sheet.

## Done when
As in the shared brief:
- within about 5% of your measured targets;
- appealing in `portrait.png`;
- the outfit and accessories reading well in `game.png` from both 45° yaws;
- ≤ 30k triangles, one atlas, `joints.json`.
