# Asset brief: kero-toy (the Toybox Kero, phase 1: the model)

Read `../villager-toy-common.md` first: the shared technical rules, the lessons and the process rules.

## Who
**Kero**, Blossom Hollow's frog: "guardian of the koi pond; very serious about lily pads". Earnest, a bit pompous in a
sweet way. The blueprint is `refs/sheet.png` ("A · POND GUARDIAN"), approved by the owner.

## Must keep (from the sheet)
- **Skin** #8AD86A, smooth vinyl; **belly** #E8F8C8: a big pale oval from the chest to the hips.
- **The head** is a wide frog head (about 1.35 : 1) with **two big rounded eye domes** on top: the eyes sit high in
  them, looking forward and a little outward. There are no ears. The domes are part of one smooth skull (no seams,
  no creases where they meet the head; that crease is what broke the procedural kit's version).
- **Eyes**: big and glossy, sclera #FFF8E9, iris #E8A23B, pupil #242522, two catchlights, the thick upper lash line with
  a wing, and soft green brows on the domes.
- **Face**: two small nostril dots #467C38; a **wide, gentle smile line** across most of the face width; blush #F0A08A.
- **Scarf** #E8503A: a thick soft roll round the neck with a front knot of two rounded ends, and a **lily-pad badge**
  #4F963F with its notch on the knot. Thick shapes only.
- **Body**: round and pear-shaped, short arms with rounded three-finger hands, short legs, **broad flat frog feet** with
  three rounded toes.

## The director's rough measurements (cross-check yours, Step 0)
On HEAD FRONT (W = head width at the cheeks):
- the head height (chin to the top of the eye domes) about 0.78 W; the domes about 0.36 W wide each, their tops about
  0.18 W above the head's middle top;

- nostrils at about 0.40 W above the chin, 0.10 W apart;
- mouth: a smile line about 0.55 W wide at about 0.27 W above the chin;
- blush about 0.14 × 0.09 W at x = ±0.38 W.
