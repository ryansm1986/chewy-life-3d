# Concept brief: tanuki-bandit-concept (Toybox Chibi, an enemy of the Burrow)

## The character
**Tanuki Bandit**: a cheeky little raccoon-dog thief, a ranged enemy of the Burrow (the game's first dungeon) and of
Danzaburō's tanuki gang in Momiji Hollow. It walks and runs like the heroes (it is rigged on the same skeleton), throws
acorns at you from range and dodges about. About **1.0 m tall** in the game world. The game's Toybox Chibi style: the
attached `sheet-B.png` (the approved Chewy) and `chewy-toy-game.png` (him in the game); the enemy series so far:
`approved-series-example-kuri.png`. **The Tanuki Bandit must look like the same toy line**, as if he were sold next to
Chewy, as a cute baddie. Deliver **3 design options**.

## Style (fixed for every option: match sheet-B)
- A vinyl-toy / Pokopia / Animal Crossing feel: about 2.4 heads tall; a big rounded head; a stubby rounded body and
  limbs; mitten hands; broad rounded feet. Soft matte surfaces, fur as a few big smooth sculpted masses.
- The face language of sheet-B (big glossy low-set eyes with catchlights and a thick lash line, a small nose, blush),
  here **cheeky and mischievous** (a smirk, a sly brow).
- Clean colour blocking, about 5–7 colours with hex swatches. Reads at 45° from 20 m.

## Identity (every option must keep these)
- A **tanuki**: brown fur (#8a6a58), a cream muzzle and belly (#e0cbb0), the dark **bandit eye mask** patches and dark
  ears and legs (#3a2c28), a big round **ringed tail**.
- A dark ninja-ish **gi** (base #4a4a6a; the second colourway a maroon #6a3a3a) with dark trousers (#2a2a3a) and a red
  **scarf / neckerchief** (#e8503a) tied bandit-style.
- A little **acorn pouch** at the belt (he throws acorns: show one in his hand as a callout).
- Personality: a sneaky, giggly little rascal who is all bluster.

## The three options (the same style, genuinely different designs)
- **Option A, "Acorn Bandit"**: the current idea perfected: the gi, the red scarf, the bandit mask, a bag of acorns.
- **Option B, "Leaf Ninja"**: a little tanuki ninja: a hood and a leaf tucked behind one ear (a nod to Danzaburō's
  transformation leaf), a sling for the acorns.
- **Option C, "Roadside Rascal"**: a straw-hatted travelling thief with a furoshiki bundle on his back, the scarf over his
  nose; the most bandit.

## Sheet requirements
One landscape sheet per option: an **A-pose turnaround** (front, 3/4, side, back, consistent), a head close-up (front
and 3/4) with 2–3 expressions (cheeky, hurt, dizzy), the acorn-throw pose and the second colourway (maroon gi) as small
callouts, hex swatches. Lay it out like sheet-B on a light, flat background. Label "TANUKI BANDIT · Option A/B/C · <name>".
