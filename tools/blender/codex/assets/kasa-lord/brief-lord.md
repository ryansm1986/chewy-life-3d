# Model brief: kasa-lord (enemy series EN-1, the Fox Shrine's boss)

The same contract and one-piece note as `brief.md` (a single `body` object, origin at the ground centre, vertex colours
only, `toon` material; the game lights the pink tongue by its colour). Build **Lord Karakasa** from the **callout on the
approved sheet** (`refs/sheet.png`: the Lord Karakasa front and 3/4), on top of your approved kasa-obake (the same
canopy, eye, lash and tongue style).

## What it is
The boss of the Fox Shrine: a huge, lordly umbrella yōkai. He hops, charges, and **spins like a top** for his big attack
(the game spins the whole model about its vertical axis). The game scales the model **×2.6** (about **4.3 m** in the
game), so build it about **1.65 m** tall to the tip of the crown; one rigid piece.

## Look (follow the sheet's callout)
- The canopy in crimson **#c8364a** (pleat valleys darker), with a **second skirt tier** of canopy below the first (the
  sheet's two tiers).
- **Gold tassels** (#d5aa45) hanging from the rim ribs of both tiers: chunky rounded tassels (≥ 4 cm thick, a knot and
  a rounded fringe lump), not strings.
- A little gold **crown** round the finial, and a big paper **ofuda** talisman (cream with a red mark) hanging on the brow
  above the eye.
- The big eye with a heavy lidded brow (lordly), and an **extra-long royal tongue** down to the floor (≥ 20 cm wide, a
  rounded tip; the game lights it, keep #ef719d).
- The wooden leg and geta as on your kasa-obake, a little sturdier.

## Budget and file
≤ 7,000 triangles. One GLB: `D:/projects/chewy-life-3d/public/models/kasa-lord.glb`.

## Done when
Matches the callout (front and 3/4); reads at the 45° game camera at 12 m and 24 m (at the boss's ×2.6 size): the two
tiers, the tassels, the crown and the tongue clear; spinning (render it at 4 yaw angles) it reads from every side.
