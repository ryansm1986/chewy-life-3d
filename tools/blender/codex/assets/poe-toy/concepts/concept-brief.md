# Concept brief: pug-concept (Toybox Chibi)

## The character
**The Pug Ninja** is the game's third playable hero, a new **ninja class** next to Moka the mage and Chewy, who is being
restyled as a **samurai** in a separate design pass. The Pug Ninja is the samurai's opposite: **swift, light and hidden**.
They're a master of **ninjutsu**:
- hand seals;
- smoke-bomb vanishes and smoke clones;
- shadow-steps that blink them behind a foe;
- a substitution trick that leaves a chew-toy behind;
- kunai and shuriken thrown in quick flurries.

They're earnest and a little dramatic, and sure they're perfectly stealthy, but they snort, snuffle and sneeze at the worst
moments (they're a pug).
The name comes later, so label the sheets **"PUG NINJA · Option A/B/C"**. Keep the character unisex: no extra lashes beyond
sheet-B's lash line.

They're a **biped like Chewy**, about **0.95–1.0 m to the top of the head** in the game world. The player switches between
them, Chewy and Moka, so they stand side by side all the time.

The game has a **Toybox Chibi** art style:
- the Chewy model sheet `sheet-B.png` and `chewy-toy-game-crop.png` (him in the game);
- Moka's sheet `moka-sheet.png`;
- Rosie's sheet `rosie-sheet-A.png`;
- Shadow's sheet `shadow-sheet-A.png`;
- `cast-lineup.png`: the toy cast in the game's light and camera.

**The Pug Ninja must look like the same toy line**, as if all of them were sold together. Deliver **3 design options**. The
owner picks one, and it's then modelled in Blender.

## Style (fixed for every option: match sheet-B and the other sheets)
- A vinyl-toy / Pokopia / Animal Crossing feel.
  - **Proportions**: about 2.4 heads tall; a big rounded head (a squircle, slightly wider than tall); a **compact,
    nimble** body: still the toy proportions and a round pug barrel chest, but lighter and springier-looking than Chewy,
    light on the toes, with a ready stance; mitten paws; broad, rounded feet.
  - **Surfaces**: soft, matte and smooth, with gentle painted shading. Fur is **a few big sculpted, smooth masses**, never
    strands.
- The **face language of sheet-B**, on a pug:
  - **big, round glossy eyes**: a warm-white sclera showing white crescents, the iris tucked toward the nose, a large
    dark pupil, two catchlights, and a thick upper lash line with a small wing;
  - the eyes face a little outward, set into the curve of the head;
  - the eyes sit **above the muzzle, mid-face**: the iris bottom at or above the top of the nose, never down on the
    cheeks;
  - soft arched brows;
  - a gentle closed smile;
  - blush dabs.
- **The pug face, as a toy**:
  - **Muzzle**: the shortest in the cast. A soft, wide, rounded "bun" muzzle that barely projects (much flatter than
    Chewy's), with chubby cheek pads either side.
  - **Nose**: a wide, rounded black nose button at the top of the muzzle, with a highlight.
  - **Mouth**: the mouth line just under the nose.
  - **Wrinkles**: **one or two big soft sculpted rolls** on the forehead and over the nose bridge, as smooth clay folds
    with painted shading. **Never thin lines or many creases.**
  - **Mask**: a dark muzzle mask, like a real fawn pug's. The fur around the eyes stays coat-coloured so the eye whites
    read; don't put dark rings around the eyes.
- **Pug ears**: small, soft, folded "rose/button" ears at the top corners of the head, flopped forward. They're chunky
  little shapes, never thin flaps, and dark on the lighter coats.
- **Pug tail**: a chunky, tight **curl** (a soft round swirl) high on the rump. Show it clearly in the side and back views.
- Clean colour blocking with crisp borders; a simple palette of about 6–8 colours, with hex swatches on the sheet.
- It must read from the game camera (45° down, about 20 m away), with a strong silhouette, big shapes and no thin
  details. **A hood or hat must never hide the eyes from 45° above.**

## Identity (every option must keep these)
- **A pug**: a flat bun muzzle with a mask, a forehead roll, round eyes, small folded ears, a curly tail and a stocky
  body.
- **A swift ninja, not a samurai**: a close-fitting, light shinobi outfit (no bulky layers), split-toe **tabi** boots, and
  wrapped arm guards or shin wraps. **No samurai cues**:
  - no hakama;
  - no katana or long sword;
  - no armour plates or kabuto;
  - no topknot;
  - no wide straw hat.
- **Signature item: bone shuriken.** Four rounded **bone-end tips** around a centre ring (a dog-themed shuriken), kept in a
  small pouch or bandolier on the costume. Also show a single shuriken as a callout.
- **The main weapon is a separate game prop.** It's held in the hands or stowed on the back in play, so:
  - show it only as a callout;
  - draw the turnaround **without it in the hands**, the paws soft closed mittens that could grip it;
  - something stowed on the back (a sheath) is fine.
- **Not Chewy.**
  - Chewy is a chocolate dog with a white chin and chest blaze. Today he wears a **navy gi, a red scarf and sash, white arm
    wraps and dark pants**, with a bone sword. He's becoming a **samurai**, with a katana-like bone sword and samurai dress.
  - The Pug Ninja must not use **navy + red**, and must not use white arm wraps as the main look.
  - The two should read as opposites at a glance: **samurai** (upright, formal, one long blade) against **ninja** (low,
    quick, hidden, small blades and smoke).
- **Not Shadow.** Shadow is a black-and-white Boston terrier with tall upright bat ears. The pug has small **folded** ears,
  a curly tail and a coat that's one colour with a dark mask.
- **Not Moka's palette.** No seafoam with lavender as the main colours.
- **Personality**: earnest, a little dramatic, proud of their stealth, and comically un-stealthy (snorts and sneezes). Show
  it in the expressions.

## The three options (the same style, genuinely different designs, and a different coat each)
- **Option A, "Shinobi Classic"**: a **fawn** pug.
  - **Coat**: fur #e2b884, light fur #f2d6a8, and a black-brown mask and ears #2e2628. Warm brown eyes (#a0602a).
  - **Costume**: a charcoal shinobi outfit (#3c3446) with plum / wine accents (#7a3a5a).
    - A soft **hood** worn up, framing the whole face, with the folded ears poking out through two slits.

    - Slate arm guards and shin wraps; charcoal tabi.
    - A bone-shuriken pouch on the sash.
  - **Main weapon callout**: twin **paw-claw tekko-kagi**, short three-prong claws on a wrapped grip worn over the paws: a

  - **Main weapon callout**: the giant fūma bone shuriken, thrown like a boomerang, drawn folded and open.

## Practical constraints (they're animated in the game)
- They run, dash, roll, flip, strike with small blades or claws, throw with one arm, and make hand seals with both paws. The costume must allow that:
  - the pants and legs are clearly visible;
  - nothing long trails on the ground or wraps the legs;
  - headband tails, scarf ends and cords are **short and chunky** (no longer than shoulder length);
  - nothing covers the arms' swing.
- **The arms and legs stay visible**: chunky toy limbs, like Chewy's.
- A hood, kasa or headband sits **firmly on the head** as one rigid shape. The folded ears stay visible.
- The mask is **down** in the turnaround and the head close-ups: the pug face is the appeal.

## Sheet requirements
One landscape sheet per option, laid out **like sheet-B**:
- an **A-pose turnaround** in **front, 3/4, side and back** views, consistent with each other, on a common ground line;
- a **head close-up**, front and 3/4, with **3 expressions**:
  - **happy** (eyes closed in a smile);
  - **stealth mode** (the face mask pulled **up** over the muzzle, eyes narrowed and focused; the one place the mask is up);
  - **oops** (a sheepish squint with a little snort or sneeze puff);
- two **action pose** callouts:
  - a **ninjutsu hand seal**: both paws pressed together in a seal at the chest, eyes focused, a smoke puff curling around
    the feet;
  - a **swift dash**: low and leaning forward, with a smoke-puff afterimage of the pug behind;
- the **main weapon** and **one bone shuriken** as small callouts;
- hex swatches;
- a light, flat, evenly lit background.

## Done when
- 3 sheets, `concepts/option-A.png`, `option-B.png` and `option-C.png`, plus `concepts/notes.md` (each option's idea and
  palette, and anything that was hard to keep consistent).
- Each one reads unmistakably as a **swift pug ninja**: the flat bun muzzle with a mask, a forehead roll, round eyes,
  folded ears, a curly tail, and light ninja gear with **no samurai cues**. Each also reads as the same toy line as Chewy, Moka, Rosie and Shadow, and is clearly
  **not Chewy's** navy-and-red gi look or a samurai.
- The three are clearly different designs, each with its own coat (fawn, black, apricot).
- The four views agree well enough to model from. The ears, the tail, the headgear and the legs must be clear in the side
  and back views.
