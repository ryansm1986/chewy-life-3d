# Pug Ninja — Toybox Chibi concept options

Three landscape model sheets made with the built-in image-generation tool. Source style references: Chewy sheet-B and game crop, Moka, Rosie, Shadow, and the cast lineup. Full generation and refinement prompts are recorded in [prompt-set.md](prompt-set.md).

Each sheet includes four neutral A-pose views on a common ground line, front and three-quarter head closeups, happy/stealth/oops expressions, hand-seal and dash actions, weapon callouts, a four-bone-tip shuriken, and labelled hex swatches. Weapons are absent from turnaround hands. C carries its permitted stowed back prop.

## Consistency and practical limits
- The difficult details were eye height above the flat muzzle, broad smooth forehead rolls, smooth coat surfaces, and the relative size of C's back prop. Targeted refinement passes addressed these.
- Heights, head ratios and C's 0.45 m prop span are design targets, not calibrated measurements extracted from the generated pixels. Model to the stated dimensions.
- Generated turnarounds have small shading and perspective differences. Use front/back to establish symmetry and the side for shallow muzzle depth; keep eyes above the nose, one broad forehead roll, folded ears and a tight tail coil.
- C's folded shuriken and hinge are a concept arrangement, not an engineered folding mechanism. Its mounting, fold operation and arm clearance need resolution in the model.
- These are concept sheets only. Actual visibility from the game's 45-degree camera and animation clearances have not been tested in Blender or the game.
- No requested panel type was intentionally omitted. Faces stay uncovered except in the stealth expression.

## Option D — Bamboo Shinobi (black), owner's Round 2 pick

**File:** [option-D.png](option-D.png)  
**Idea:** Option B's black pug in Option C's complete green Bamboo Shinobi costume, with C's side festival mask and back-mounted folding bone shuriken.  
**Proportions:** Target 1.00 m to top of bare head and 2.4 heads tall; compact, nimble body, broad squircle head, mitten paws, folded black ears and tight black curl tail high on the rump.

**Palette**
- Black fur: #2E2A30
- Cool sheen/toy pads: #4A4450
- Subtle muzzle mask, folded ears and nose: #1E1B20
- Honey-gold eyes: #C88A3A
- Moss wrapped top, lowered hood/cowl and tabi: #5A8A4A
- Cream collar, lowered face mask, wraps, festival mask and bone props: #F4EAD2
- Mustard scarf, sash knot, mask ties and shuriken hinge/mount: #D8B040
- Dark moss puffy shorts, guards, pouch and festival-mask markings: #3D6038

**Difference:** This is the selected B/C combination, rather than a fourth independent costume direction. B supplies the black coat, honey eyes, clear white crescents, soft forehead roll, shallow bun muzzle and small closed smile. C supplies the complete bamboo outfit, side festival mask, small jutsu scroll, hip pouch and giant folding fūma shuriken. There is no headband, crescent badge, blue trim, silver gear, bandolier or kunai.

**Readability decision:** Inspected the full exported sheet, a 960 × 640 preview and a 384 × 256 thumbnail. Bright cream borders at the face/neck and mustard accents separate the black head from the moss torso. The moss remains #5A8A4A; the optional lift toward #66A055 was not used. No other palette target was changed. Hex swatches specify base-material targets; the illustrated surfaces include painted lighting and shading.

**Modelling notes:** Keep the festival mask and scroll on character left, and the bone-shuriken pouch on character right, matching C. The four turnaround views share a ground line and show the same costume and stowed prop. The back view exposes the cowl above the mustard centre-ring mount and the black curl tail below; the profile shows mount depth and the tail outside the outfit. The giant shuriken retains the 0.45 m design target and separate open/folded callouts. The cream face mask is lowered everywhere except the stealth expression. Both action poses carry the back prop.

**Export and review:** The final sheet is a 1536 × 1024 RGB PNG, generated with the built-in image-generation tool and copied into the project. [Preview](option-D-preview.png) and [thumbnail](option-D-thumbnail.png) were re-rendered from that saved PNG and visually inspected. The exact generation prompt is recorded in [option-D-prompt.md](option-D-prompt.md).

**Consistency limits:** As with the original sheets, slight view-dependent scale, perspective and shading differences remain in this generated concept art. Use the stated dimensions and shared anatomy when modelling; this is not a calibrated orthographic drawing. The folded weapon is a visual concept, not a resolved mechanical design. Game-camera visibility and animation clearances have not been tested in Blender or the game.

**Originals preserved:** SHA-256 comparisons confirm that option-A.png, option-B.png and option-C.png are unchanged by Round 2.
