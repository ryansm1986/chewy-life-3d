`concepts/option-D.png` ("PUG NINJA · Option D, Bamboo Shinobi (black)"), and add a section for it to `concepts/notes.md`.
Leave A, B and C untouched.

## Take from Option B (`option-B.png`), unchanged
Everything about the pug: the black coat and face.
- **Colours**: fur #2E2A30, the cool sheen and toy pads #4A4450, the subtle muzzle mask, ears and nose #1E1B20, and
  **honey-gold eyes #C88A3A**.

## Readability on the black coat
Moss green on a black pug has less contrast than B's cream. Keep C's **cream collar, cream lowered mask and mustard
scarf** bright and clearly bordered around the face and neck, so the head separates from the body at a glance. If the
moss reads too close to the black fur at thumbnail size, lift the moss a little toward #66A055 and say so in the notes.
Don't change any of the other colours.
