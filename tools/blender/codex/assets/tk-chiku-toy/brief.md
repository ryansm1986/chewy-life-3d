# Asset brief: tk-chiku-toy (VL-2: the Blender Chiku, phase 1: the model)

Read `../villager-toy-common.md` first: the shared technical rules, the lessons and the process rules. **Two differences
from it**:
- This is a **zone villager** (Takemori Village, the bamboo zone), not one of Blossom Hollow's seven.
- The sheet `refs/sheet.png` was drawn for this task (2026-11-02) and approved by the director. The owner delegated the
  pick. It is the blueprint: don't redesign, just build it beautifully.
`refs/current-*-portrait.png` (where present) is the procedural kit version in the game now: identity only.

## Who
**Chiku**, Takemori's shopkeeper, a cheerful, chatty bunny who counts bamboo shoots in her sleep.
The blueprint is `refs/sheet.png` ("Chiku - Shopkeeper").

## Must keep (from the sheet)
- **Fur** #F4EAD8; **muzzle and belly** #FFFAF4; **long upright ears** with pink inner #FFC4CC (thick, rounded volumes, never cards); a pink nose #E88DA4; a round tail.
- **Eyes** brown #6A4A2A; blush #F3A1AC.
- A **green bandana** #5A9A6A worn as a headband between the ears, tied with a rounded knot and two short thick ends at the side or back (match the sheet's views).
- **Shirt** #7CC0A0; **apron** #F6E6C4 with a front pocket; **trousers** #4A6A5A; a **tan shoulder bag** #C8A060 on a strap across the body. **The strap must be a thick, rounded band (about 4 cm), not a thin line.**

## Sizes
- Total height **1.05 m to the crown, 1.30 m with the ears** (the sheet's note). Villagers stand about 1.0–1.15 m to the crown.
- Measure everything else on the sheet in Step 0 (`measure.md`, fractions of the head width W). The director checks your
  table against the sheet.

## Done when
As in the shared brief:
- within about 5% of your measured targets;
- appealing in `portrait.png`;
- the outfit and accessories reading well in `game.png` from both 45° yaws;
- ≤ 30k triangles, one atlas, `joints.json`.
