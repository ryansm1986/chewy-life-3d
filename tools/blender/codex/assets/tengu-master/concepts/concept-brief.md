# Concept brief: tengu-master-concept (Pawhaven boss, the Whispering Bamboo Grove)

The enemy series so far: `approved-series-example-takenoko.png` and `approved-series-example-kuri.png` show the approved
style; `approved-region-in-game.jpg` shows this region's approved Blender enemies in the game (the AFTER half), the
ones this boss leads. Match their style and finish; the boss is the grand centrepiece of the set.

## The boss
**Master Tengu**, a karasu-tengu (crow tengu) master of the wind, the boss of the Bamboo Grove (and its dungeon's
hollow). About **2.5 m** tall in the game; he floats and hops on single-tooth geta.

What he does in the fight (design for this):
- **Fan gust**: he sweeps his giant feather fan (hauchiwa) and a wind wall shoves you back.
- **Tornadoes**: leaf-blade whirlwinds he whips up with the fan.
- **Dive**: he rockets up out of sight on his wings and slams down.
- **Crow disciples**: he calls little crow tengu to help.
- Between moves he takes a **breather**: fanning himself, a sweat drop (the moment to hit him).
- Phase 2: a bamboo-leaf wind storm.

Identity to keep (every option):
- A **crow face** with a hooked beak (yellow), bushy **white brows**, big eyes; a small black **tokin** cap on his brow.
- **Yamabushi** mountain-monk robes (white / indigo) with the big **pompom sash** (bonbonchi) on the chest.
- Big dark **wings** with a blue sheen, single-tooth **geta**.
- The giant **feather fan** in one hand (a round fan of big chunky feathers).
- Personality: a proud, theatrical old wind master who laughs at you (a laughing open beak).

Moving parts (separate pieces): the body (robes, legs, geta); the **head** (with the beak's lower **jaw** as its own
piece for the laugh, and closed **eyelids** for the blink); the **fan arm** (the arm and the fan, one piece, swinging
from the shoulder); the **other arm**; the two **wings** (each flaps from its shoulder root).

Signature poses (callouts): the **fan gust** (fan swept wide, wind lines); the **dive** (wings spread, plunging); the
**breather** (fanning himself, sweat drop, beak open laughing).

**Callout (also draw it): the Crow Disciple** (karasu-kozō), his little add: a tiny chubby crow tengu kid (about 0.8 m),
a small tokin, a little robe (cream #ece2ca; a second colourway pale blue #dfe8f4), two flapping wings: front + 3/4.

## The three options (the same style, genuinely different designs)
- **Option A, "Wind Master"**: the current idea perfected: a stout crow tengu in white-and-indigo yamabushi robes, big wings folded behind, the huge round feather fan.
- **Option B, "Mountain Sage"**: an older, taller sage: a long white beard below the beak, a monk's staff with rings alongside the fan, wings like a cape.
- **Option C, "Tengu Chick"**: the most chibi: a round, puffed-up crow master, a giant head, a fan bigger than he is, stubby wings; the cutest.
