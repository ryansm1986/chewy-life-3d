   - **Width**: the sheet's ear lobe at its widest (about z 0.2 W above the chin) is about **0.42–0.45 W** across; the
     model's is about 0.32–0.34 W. Measure on the same crops, and widen the lower two-thirds of each ear to match. The
     ears fan out a little as they hang: the outer edge flares, while the inner edge stays close to the cheek.
   - **Thickness and edge**: the sheet's ears have a soft **rolled edge** (a rounded rim, about 0.04 W thick) and a
     gentle concave inner face with the pink fold showing, so they read as plush, soft volumes. The model's read as
     smooth flat panels, especially the near ear in 3/4. Give them a rolled rim and a soft inner dish, keeping the
     pink.
   - **The root crease**: in the front view a dark sliver shows between each ear's top and the skull. On the sheet the
     ear root wraps smoothly onto the head, with no gap. Close or blend the seam: shade it softly, without a black line.
   - **Keep**: the ear length (the bottom at −0.349 W), the high root position from fix 2 of round 1, and the far ear's
     lower top.
2. **The dress silhouette should flare like a bell.** On the sheet's front view, the robe flares from the obi into a
   wide A-line: the periwinkle hem is about **1.45× the obi's width**, and the pink petticoat under it about **1.55×**.
   The model's skirt hangs nearly straight, about 1.15–1.2×.
   - Measure both widths on `cmp-front`, and flare the skirts to match, so the whole dress reads as a jellyfish bell.
   - Keep the lengths you set in round 2 (the periwinkle at 62%, the pink layer's height).
   - The lobes stay thick and round, and the hems must clear the feet in a walk (step about 0.25 m).
3. **Nose colour**: it's about 8% lighter than the target, which is fine. Leave it.
