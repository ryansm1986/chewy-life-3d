# Asset brief: kitsune-toy (the Toybox Kitsune, phase 1: the model)

Read `../villager-toy-common.md` first: the shared technical rules, the lessons and the process rules.

## Who
**Kitsune**, Blossom Hollow's shrine keeper: "a mysterious shrine keeper who hums old songs". Calm, kind, a little
knowing. The blueprint is `refs/sheet.png` ("KITSUNE · SHRINE SONG"), approved by the owner.

## Must keep (from the sheet)
- **Fur** #FF9A4A; **white** #FFF4E8 on the lower face (cheeks and muzzle, with soft pointed cheek tufts at the sides),
  the chest and the tail's tip.
- **Face**: big amber eyes (iris #E6AD3F, darker toward the top) with the lash line and wing; soft brows; a short
  rounded fox muzzle (gentle, not a long snout) with a dark nose #493044 and a wide gentle smile; blush #FFA3A0.
  Keep the muzzle short: in profile it projects only a little past the cheeks.
- **Ears**: tall pointed fox ears with rounded tips, inner ears #FFBEAC, the upper third dark #493044. Thick, from both
  45° yaws.
- **Kimono top** #6A5AD0 with a white crossed collar and short wide sleeves; **sash** #FF5A6A with a gold **bell**
  #F2C451 at the front (a round bell with a slot, thick); a **pleated hakama skirt** #3A3060 to above the feet (big,
  soft pleats, not thin ridges).
- **Tail**: a big fluffy fox tail, a smooth teardrop mass with a white tip, curving out behind (a separate piece with
  bend loops). It's large on the sheet: keep its volume.
- **Body**: small and round, mitten paws in orange, rounded feet with toe lines.

## The director's rough measurements (cross-check yours, Step 0)
On HEAD FRONT (W = head width at the cheeks including the tufts, ears excluded):
- head height (crown to chin) about 0.70 W;
- eyes: centres at x = ±0.225 W, about 0.40 W above the chin; opening about 0.22 W wide × 0.25 W tall;
- nose about 0.09 W wide; mouth about 0.22 W wide, the smile corners curling up;
- blush about 0.13 × 0.08 W at x = ±0.33 W;
- ears: about 0.30 W wide at the base, rising about 0.50 W above the crown.
- Total height 1.10 m.

## Done when
As in the shared brief: within about 5% of your measured targets, appealing in `portrait.png`, the ears, the bell and
the tail reading well in `game.png` from both 45° yaws, ≤ 30k triangles, one atlas, `joints.json`.
