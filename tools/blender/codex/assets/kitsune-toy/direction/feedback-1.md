## Fixes
1. **The ears (the main one).** They read as folded paper: thin shells whose rims show orange and dark stripes, with the
   lower outer corners flaring off the head. From the game camera the near ear looks like a bent card. On the sheet
   they're big, thick, soft triangles. Rebuild them as **solid volumes**:
   - thickness about 0.07 W at the base, tapering to about 0.03 W near the tip, with a rounded tip (radius about
     0.03 W) and rounded edges all round, no knife rims;
   - base width about 0.32 W, the bases on the top corners of the head and blending smoothly into it, with no flare
     past the head's outline at the bottom;
   - the front face a shallow cup filled with the **broad inner-ear triangle** #FFBEAC (about 60% of the front face,
     as on the sheet), its edge 0.04 W inside the rim;
   - the **dark top** #493044 painted as one clean band over the top third on both faces and the rims, its lower edge a
     soft straight-ish line, as on the sheet (no streaks down the edges);
   - each ear turned about 15–20° outward. Check them from both 45° yaws (`game-45.png`) and in profile: in the side view
     an ear should read as a thick triangle, not a sliver.
2. **The lower face.** The white lower face bulges into jowls (the head front and the portrait show a pouch under the
   mouth and puffy cheeks). On the sheet the white is a smooth area on the same convex surface as the rest of the face.
   Reduce the lower cheek mass so the jaw's outline follows the sheet's (your outline table stays within 0.01 W), keep
   the side cheek tufts, and blend the muzzle so no break shows under the mouth.
3. **The mouth line.** Thin and wiggly now. Make it the sheet's smooth wide smile: one clean curve from under the nose
   out to gently curled corners, the line about 0.012 W thick, #493044.
4. **The eyes' paint.** Pupils near-black (#1E1418, not brown), as big as the sheet's (your table: 0.092 × 0.136 W),
   and the upper lash line about 25% thicker, with the sheet's wing.
5. **The sleeves.** From the side you look into a hollow ring. Make them closed puffs, like the sheet: the opening
   faces down and back, its inside dark, and the forearm fills it so there's no visible hollow from any view.
6. **The tail.** About 15% smaller, to the sheet's size and reach (its profile: 0.18–0.53 m behind the axis, 0.13–0.63 m
   high).
