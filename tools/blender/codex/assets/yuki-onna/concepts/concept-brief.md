# Concept brief: yuki-onna-concept (Pawhaven boss, Yukimi Onsen)

The enemy series so far: `approved-series-example-takenoko.png` and `approved-series-example-kuri.png` show the approved
style; `approved-region-in-game.jpg` shows this region's approved Blender enemies in the game (the AFTER half), the
ones this boss leads. Match their style and finish; the boss is the grand centrepiece of the set.

## The boss
**Yuki-onna, the Frost Princess**, the snow woman of folklore, the boss of Yukimi Onsen (and its caverns' hall). About
**2.9 m** tall in the game; she floats a little above the ice with an icy glow round her.

What she does in the fight (design for this):
- **Blizzard**: she breathes in (cheeks puffed, sleeves up) and blows a cone of snow.
- **Icicles**: a sleeve raised, icicles fall around you.
- **Frozen floor**: she twirls and glazes the floor with ice.
- **Ice mirrors**: she vanishes and steps out of one of several mirrors; **glass copies** of her (the same model in a
  glassy blue material) step out of the others.
- She calls snow children. Phase 2: a whiteout.

Identity to keep (every option):
- An elegant, **cute** snow spirit: a pale **furisode** kimono that melts into icy blue at the hem, with **big flat
  snowflake shapes** and a wave band near the hem (big shapes only: the model has no textures), a trailing train.
- An ice-blue **obi** with a crystal brooch and a big **bow** at the back.
- Long black **hime-cut** hair (blunt fringe, side locks, long back), big calm **blue eyes**, a small mouth (an "o" for the
  breath), blush; an ice **tiara** and a snowflake **kanzashi**.
- A frosty **shawl** ribbon (hagoromo) floating round her.
- Personality: serene, a little haughty, secretly playful.

Moving parts (separate pieces): the body (kimono, obi, bow); the **head** (face, hair front); the **back hair** (its own
piece: it sways); the **face** features on their own (thin outline); the closed **eyelids** (blink); the **"o" mouth** and
**puffed cheeks** (the breath); the two long **sleeves** (each swings from the shoulder); the **shawl** ribbon.

Signature poses (callouts): the **blizzard breath** (cheeks puffed, sleeves up); the **icicle call** (a sleeve raised); the
**twirl** (sleeves and shawl flying); a **glass copy** of her (the same figure in clear icy blue glass).

## The three options (the same style, genuinely different designs)
- **Option A, "Frost Princess"**: the current idea perfected: the pale furisode fading to icy blue, the long black hime-cut hair, the tiara, the floating shawl.
- **Option B, "Snow Lantern Lady"**: a hooded snow-veil (wataboshi) over the hair, carrying a small snow lantern, a longer trailing hem like drifting snow.
- **Option C, "Little Frost Hime"**: the most chibi: a big round head and huge eyes, a short puffy kimono, a big bow; the cutest.
