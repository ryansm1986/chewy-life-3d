# Concept brief: wisp-concept (Pawhaven enemy series, the Burrow)

The series so far: the owner approved `approved-series-example-takenoko.png` (Bamboo Grove) and
`approved-series-example-kuri.png` (Momiji Hollow). Match their style, finish and sheet layout.

The Burrow is the game's first dungeon: four little dens under the village (the Mossy Burrow, the Fox Shrine, the Oni
Kitchen, the Moon Sanctum). Its monsters are the first enemies a young player meets, so they are the simplest and
friendliest-looking of the series.

**This kind is one rigid piece** (the game bobs, squashes, tilts and spins the whole model; nothing on it moves on its
own), so design it as one solid toy figure: no separate swinging parts needed.

## The monster
**Kitsune-bi**, a fox-fire wisp, the quick zap caster of the Burrow. Packs of 3–8. About **1.45 m** off the ground at
the top, floating; the flame body about 0.6 m tall.

What it does in a fight: it **floats** and darts, and **fires bolts of foxfire** at you.

Identity to keep (every option): a soft **teardrop flame** (blue #7ab8ff, paler at the core) with a curled tip, little
**fox ears** of flame, a sweet sleepy fox face, a wisp tail. The game makes it glow softly (design clean flat colour).

Variant strip (same model, colourways): 1. blue #7ab8ff; 2. violet #b88aff.

## The three options (the same style, genuinely different designs)
- **Option A, "Foxfire Drop"**: the current idea perfected: a teardrop flame with fox ears and a fox face.
- **Option B, "Fox Spirit"**: a little flame fox head with a puffy flame ruff and three flame tails.
- **Option C, "Spark Bub"**: the most chibi: a round flame bean with tiny ears and a huge face; the cutest.
